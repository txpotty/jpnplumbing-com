    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Newport East

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. It finds rebar and cables. Combined with a listening device, radar finds leaks in any pipe type, even with traffic noise, since radar maps the route and the mic confirms the spot. We use both in Newport East.

    Slab Leaks in Stores and Offices in Newport East

    Retail stores, offices and restaurants on slabs develop the same leaks as houses. Tenants feel it fast. We locate and repair commercial slab leaks with as little disruption as we can manage, often after hours, and we provide written documentation with photos for property managers and insurers.

    Slab Leak Detection in Newport East

    The first test is the water meter. We shut off everything first. If the meter still turns, we close the water heater inlet and pressure test hot and cold separately, then walk the losing line with a ground mic until the hiss is loudest. Thermal imaging shows hot water leaks as warm areas on the floor.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Newport East, RI

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Expansion wears them out. Each time hot water runs, the pipe expands against concrete and gravel, and recirculation pumps keep that cycle going all day, which grinds the pipe wall thin at the rub points. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Newport East, RI

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. That is when a repipe pays off. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Newport East homes.
