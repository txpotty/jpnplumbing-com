    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in Newport East

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. Both distances matter. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Services in Newport East, RI

    Businesses in Newport East, RI with steady hot water use, like salons, restaurants and small gyms, often get more out of two or three tankless units racked together than one big tank.     Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Newport East

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump.
