    Tub and Shower Combo Installation in Newport East

    Setting a tub is mostly about level and support.     Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. They suit guest baths well. Usually a one-day job.
