    What Our Gas Line Plumbers Fix in Newport East, RI

    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Newport East, RI

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Older uncoated brass connectors can crack at the flare nut, and they are no longer accepted by most codes or utilities. New coated connectors go in. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Black Iron Pipe Repair in Newport East, RI

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Vibration and settling loosen threaded joints over time, and pipe in damp garages or outside walls can rust through from the surface. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Newport East, RI

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. Wrong orifices make a burner run dirty and unsafe. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Newport East, RI

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. Sizing is the first thing we check. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Newport East, RI

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. Each new appliance gets a properly sized branch, a shutoff you can reach, a drip leg where required and a new coated connector. Leaking existing lines get repaired during the same trip.

    Emergency Gas Line Repair in Newport East

    If you smell gas, leave the building, skip the light switches and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Then call us. After the gas company secures the meter, our plumber finds the leak, repairs it and runs the test the utility needs to see before turning gas back on. Pilots get relit once gas is back. The truck carries enough pipe and fittings to finish most emergency jobs in one trip.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Newport East

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. Leaking joints get taken apart, cleaned, resealed with gas-rated compound and tightened, and damaged fittings or valves get replaced. We soap-test the repaired joint and pressure test the system before leaving.
