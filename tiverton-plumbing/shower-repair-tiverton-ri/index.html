    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Tiverton, RI

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Moen Posi-Temp showers take the 1222, and Delta Monitor 13 and 14 showers take the RP46074, and both wear out on a fairly regular clock. Both ride on our trucks. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Tiverton

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Emergency Shower Repair in Tiverton, RI

    A shower that will not shut off is an emergency. Close the main valve where the water line enters the house, or the shower's own stops if it has them behind an access panel. Next, call us. JPN Plumbing sends a plumber with cartridges on the truck, and we can often get your water back on within the hour.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Tiverton

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. Fresh thread tape fixes it. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Tiverton, RI

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. Each part gets tested. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Commercial Shower Service in Tiverton

    Hotels and dorms cannot wait days on a shower. With commercial cartridges on board, we fix rooms one at a time so the rest stay open. We can work nights. We set and test the limit stop on each valve before the next room.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Tiverton, RI

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. We start with the head. If every fixture is weak, we check the regulator and the main.
