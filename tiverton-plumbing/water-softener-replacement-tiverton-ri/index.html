    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Tiverton, RI

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Obsolete valves are another story.

    Residential Water Softener Replacement in Tiverton

    Scale in the water heater is the expensive part of hard water. Scale settles on the bottom of a tank heater and insulates the water from the burner, and tankless heaters clog even faster. Soft water prevents it.

    Commercial Water Softener Replacement in Tiverton

    Restaurants and laundromats run softeners hard, and a commercial unit often needs a 1.5-inch or 2-inch valve and twin tanks to keep up with flow. Peak flow drives the sizing. We work around your business hours.

    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Tiverton

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. It mounts on the main line after any filter and needs no electricity or drain. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Replacement in Tiverton, RI

    Salt-free conditioners, sometimes sold as descalers, change how hardness minerals behave without removing them, so the water still tests hard. They do keep scale from sticking inside pipes and water heaters. No salt, no drain. Swapping to a salt-based unit is an option too.

    Metered Softener Replacement in Tiverton

    Timer softeners regenerate every few days whether you used 50 gallons or 500. A metered valve, Fleck 5600SXT or Clack WS1, tracks gallons and regenerates when the resin actually needs it. Salt bills usually drop in the first month.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Tiverton, RI

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. We set it before we leave.

    Softener Loop Installation in Tiverton, RI

    Softener loops are the pair of stubbed-out lines near the garage wall or water heater where a softener ties into the main. We can add a loop with shutoff valves and a bypass. Outdoor spigots stay on hard water. The drain goes to a laundry box or floor drain with an air gap.

    Softener Replacement Near You in Tiverton, RI

    A local plumber can also check how the softener ties into your water heater and main line. Leaking loops, missing air gaps and bypass valves left half open all hurt performance, and we catch them. Quotes are straightforward.

    Local water varies. City water and well water a few miles apart can test very differently, and iron or chlorine changes the setup. The system matches your test, not a guess.
