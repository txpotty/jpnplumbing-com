    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Tiverton, RI

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. A jet scours the entire pipe wall. Snaking suits a one-time clog, while jetting fixes a line that clogs repeatedly.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in Tiverton

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers need drains that keep up with heavy use every day. A backup closes rooms or stores. We set up jetting plans for each building's needs, schedule work during slow hours, and keep the camera footage on file so property managers can show tenants or owners the condition of the lines.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Tiverton, RI

    Older cast iron drains corrode and build scale on the inner wall, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. A jet clears that crust. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in Tiverton

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Hard Water Scale Removal in Tiverton, RI

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a frequent sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. Scouring nozzles remove the scale, and badly scaled cast iron may get a descaling pass too. You can see the change on video.

    Storm and Yard Drain Jetting in Tiverton, RI

    Yard drains, channel drains and downspout lines fill with sand, silt and leaves between storms. Heavy rain can overload older sewer systems, so backwater valves help protect basements from sewage backups. A clogged yard drain sends that water back toward the house, so we jet the lines from the outlet end and flush the catch basins before the wet season starts. We also check the pop-up emitters.

    Main Sewer Line Hydrojetting in Tiverton

    The main sewer carries every drain in the house, so a blockage there backs up the lowest fixture first. Jetting the main clears roots, grease and sludge all the way to the city connection. On a 4-inch line we use a heavier nozzle with more flow, which moves the loosened debris downstream instead of leaving it to settle in a low spot.
