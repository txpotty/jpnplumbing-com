    Post-Tension Slab Leak Detection in Tiverton

    Many newer homes sit on post-tension slabs.     Pressure Testing and Isolation in Tiverton

    Pressure isolation is always the first step. Our tech closes the main, caps or valves off the hot and cold sides, and puts a gauge on each, then watches which one drops over fifteen to thirty minutes. The gauge points us to the right line.     Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Tiverton, RI

    A supply leak and a drain leak show different signs. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, and they show up as odors, slow drains or damp floors near bathrooms. We test both kinds in your Tiverton, RI home.
