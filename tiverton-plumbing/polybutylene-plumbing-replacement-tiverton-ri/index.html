    Why Polybutylene Fails in Tiverton

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a common sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. Hot water speeds it up. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Tiverton, RI
