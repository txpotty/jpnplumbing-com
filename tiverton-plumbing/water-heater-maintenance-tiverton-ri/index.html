    Water Heater Leak Checks in Tiverton, RI

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust trails mean trouble. Catching it early saves the floor.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Tiverton

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment piles up around the lower element, it runs hotter than it should, and eventually it burns out, which is why you suddenly get only a few minutes of hot water. We test both elements. With power off, we check thermostats and connections.

    Anode Rod Replacement in Tiverton

    Some water eats anode rods fast. Depending on your water chemistry, a rod that should last five years might be spent in three, so we pull it on every visit instead of assuming it is fine because the tank is still young. Spent rods get replaced. A healthy rod keeps the tank from rusting through.

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Tiverton

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. On a standard tank visit we flush that sediment out, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, check the anode for corrosion and inspect every fitting for leaks, which protects one of the more expensive appliances in the house. The tank heats better. So does recovery time.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Tiverton, RI

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Commercial Water Heater Service in Tiverton

    Businesses cannot run without hot water. Restaurants, salons and gyms in Tiverton lean on large tanks or banks of tankless units, and a failure during business hours means closing a kitchen or sending customers home. Off-peak visits are available. Larger units get the same flush, anode and safety checks.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Tiverton

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Tiverton, RI

    Gas units need a clean burner and a good draft. Leak checks, flame inspection, burner cleaning and a draft test are part of every gas visit. CO is the risk. We also test the thermocouple.
