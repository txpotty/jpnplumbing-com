    Disc Faucet Repair in Tiverton

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. The seals are cheap.     Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Tiverton, RI

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. Cracked or discontinued faucets are different. We give you both prices so the choice is yours.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Tiverton

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. On pull-down faucets, the check valves in the hose can stick too, and we clean or replace those.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Tiverton

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper.     Dripping Faucet Solutions in Tiverton

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. Anything worn gets replaced, and the rest gets checked. Compression, cartridge, ball and disc faucets all fail differently, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Tiverton, RI
