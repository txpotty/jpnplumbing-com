    Duplex and Commercial Ejector Systems in Tiverton, RI

    Commercial buildings, multi-unit properties and busy homes may need two-pump setups with an alternating control panel. Alternating the lead pump spreads wear, and when one fails or flow spikes, the second starts on its own and the panel sounds an alarm. Two pumps, one alarm.

    Residential Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Tiverton

    Residential ejector systems in Tiverton serve basement bathrooms, lower-level suites and any fixture that sits too low to drain by gravity to the sewer. The setup is a sealed basin, a pump, a float switch, a check valve and a vent. Simple parts, exact install. Most problems come from the install, not the hardware, so we take our time on the vent and the lid seal.

    Cost-Effective Sewage Ejector Pump Upgrades in Tiverton, RI

    Upgrading an old ejector can often keep the existing pit.     Upflush and Macerating Toilet Systems in Tiverton

    Macerating systems like Saniflo add a bath fixture or two without breaking the concrete floor. The unit sits behind the toilet, grinds the waste and pumps it through a small discharge line, often three-quarter inch or one inch, up to the nearest drain, which makes it a good fit for a small basement half bath. Venting still applies.

    Grinder Pump Installation in Tiverton

    Grinder pumps chop waste into slurry before pumping it, which lets them push through small pipe over long distances. They are the right choice when the sewer is far away, uphill or under pressure, like on a pressure sewer system where the pump feeds a shared force main in the street. Grinders run pricier. For a basement bath near the drain, a standard ejector usually works.

    Laundry and Wet Bar Ejector Pumps in Tiverton, RI

    Below-grade laundry rooms and wet bars send gray water down that still needs to be lifted to the sewer. A compact gray water pump handles it, sized for the 15 or more gallons a washer drops at once, with a lint filter to keep fibers off the impeller. Filters save impellers.
