    Submersible Pump Pulling and Repair in Woonsocket

    Sand wears impellers down over time, and a pump that once filled the tank in a minute starts taking three. Other common failures are the motor, the splice and the check valve. Before pulling anything, we test the motor and wire from the wellhead. Replacement pumps get sized to the yield and depth of the well.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Woonsocket

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Most switches fail from pitted contacts, a plugged sensing tube or insects in the housing. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Well Pump Brands We Service in Woonsocket, RI

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Myers make most of the pumps we pull in this area, and JPN Plumbing services all of them. Older units and newer variable speed systems are both familiar.     Constant Pressure System Repair in Woonsocket, RI

    Variable speed drives like the Grundfos SQE and Franklin SubDrive keep pressure steady by changing motor speed. A fault code on the drive points us in the right direction. We troubleshoot the drive, the pressure sensor and the wiring, then repair or replace the failed part and verify the setpoint. These need a smaller tank.

    Well Pressure Tank Repair in Woonsocket

    Bladder tanks hold a cushion of air that lets the pump rest between cycles. A failed bladder lets the tank waterlog, and the pump begins to short cycle. We inspect the bladder, the switch and the tank connections. A properly sized tank can add years to pump life.

    Pressure Tank Problems in Woonsocket

    Short cycling is the classic pressure tank symptom, with the pump clicking on and off every few seconds while a faucet runs. Usually the bladder has failed or the tank has lost its air charge. We check the precharge with the tank drained. It should sit about 2 psi below the cut-in pressure, so a 40/60 switch wants roughly 38 psi in the tank.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Woonsocket, RI

    Electrical faults cause a big share of well calls, from a tripped breaker to a burned contact in the pressure switch. After that, we look at the capacitor and relay in the control box. Our technicians test circuits with the power locked out. All wiring and splices meet Rhode Island electrical code before power goes back on.

    Control Box & Electrical Repair in Woonsocket, RI

    Capacitors, relays and corroded terminals inside the control box cause many pump failures. A tripped breaker is often the first sign. Licensed technicians test these parts safely with the power locked out, replace what failed and tighten every connection in the box. Wiring meets Rhode Island code before we restart the pump.
