    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Woonsocket, RI

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Woonsocket

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. Install time is shorter too. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Woonsocket, RI

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. A battery backup pump sits beside the primary and runs on its own float. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Woonsocket, RI

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Woonsocket

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Woonsocket, RI

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Clearance is usually under two feet. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Woonsocket

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. We backfill around the basin with washed gravel so groundwater has a path in. Then we patch the slab. Last come the pump, the lid and the discharge line.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Installation in Woonsocket

    Most Woonsocket, RI sump pump jobs land between $600 and $1,800. That range covers a quality pump, a new check valve, the discharge fittings and the labor to set and test it in a pit that already exists. New pits cost more. We write it all down before work starts.

    Swapping a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. A new pit can take a full day.

    The pump has its manufacturer warranty, and JPN Plumbing warranties the installation labor. We spell out the terms in your quote.

    Most standard installs take three to six hours. If we have to cut the slab, dig a new pit and patch the concrete, plan on most of a day, with the patch curing overnight. A simple swap is quicker.

    A straight replacement often needs no permit. We let you know if your job needs one, usually only for new pits or wiring.
