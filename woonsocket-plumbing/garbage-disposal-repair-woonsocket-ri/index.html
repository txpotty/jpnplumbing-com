    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Woonsocket, RI

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Woonsocket, RI

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Most repairs use parts from the truck. A failed motor means a new unit.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Woonsocket

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Most smells start at the splash guard. We pull it, clean the chamber walls and replace the guard if it is torn.

    Disposal Repair vs Replacement Costs in Woonsocket, RI

    Jams and minor leaks usually cost far less than a new unit. If the disposal is past eight to ten years old, leaks from the body or has a failing motor, replacement is usually the better value, and we give you both prices when it is close. The choice is yours.

    Disposal Reset and Electrical Repair in Woonsocket

    When a disposal will not turn on at all, the thermal overload may have tripped or the switch may be bad. The reset, breaker, wall switch and wiring all get tested, along with air switches and the magnetic lids on batch-feed units. Most are simple fixes.

    Electrical Problems and Resetting in Woonsocket

    No hum at all usually means no power reaching the motor. The red reset button on the bottom is the first stop, then check the breaker, the wall switch and the outlet or junction box under the sink with a meter. It is wet under a sink. Corroded wire nuts and loose cord connections are common in that damp space, and we replace them rather than just tightening them back down.

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Woonsocket

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount first, then the chamber for debris, then listen to the motor with the chamber empty, because a motor that whines with nothing in it has bad bearings.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Woonsocket, RI

    The flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube and body are where leaks start. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the internal seal has failed, and in that case replacement is the better option. That seal cannot be replaced.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Woonsocket

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. Controls get checked first. A bad solenoid or tripped overload in the panel kills the unit as fully as a jam does, but it gets fixed far faster.

    Local Disposal Repair in Woonsocket, RI

    Local water affects disposals. Hard water leaves scale on the grind ring and the chamber walls, and knowing that helps us spot whether the problem is the unit or buildup in the line. You get a fix that lasts.
