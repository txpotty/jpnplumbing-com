    Cracked and Broken Pipe Repair in Woonsocket

    Clay and cast iron crack from settling soil, tree roots and vehicles parked over the line. The crack lets soil in and sewage out. Most of these are one-day jobs.

    Sewer Line Hydro-Jetting in Woonsocket

    A hydro-jetter pushes water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full inside of the pipe. Snaking just opens a path. That makes jetting the better choice for grease, scale and fine root growth, and it is the standard prep step before any pipe lining job. Brittle or broken pipe is a different story. We check the pipe on video before we jet it.

    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Woonsocket

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, so any excavation stays small and targeted. Older homes in Woonsocket often hide two or three problems in one line, and the video catches all of them.

    Raw sewage on a basement floor is not a next-week problem. We answer at night and on weekends.     Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Woonsocket, RI

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Usually two small pits.
