    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Woonsocket, RI

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground.     PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Woonsocket

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, and for PEX we use the matching crimp or expansion tool so the joint is made the way the manufacturer intends.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Woonsocket

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. First step is a pressure gauge on the outside spigot.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Woonsocket

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Woonsocket

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, then swap a failed regulator for a new Watts or Zurn Wilkins valve when it reads too high. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.
