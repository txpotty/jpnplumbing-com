    Common Sink Repairs in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Cumberland Hill

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. When the epoxy under the counter fails, the bowl drops a quarter inch, the caulk line splits, and water from the counter runs straight down into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Supply Line and Angle Stop Replacement in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Braided supply lines have a rubber core that ages. After five to ten years the core can split without warning, and old multi-turn stops often seize open. Lines and stops get replaced together. Quarter-turn stops close in one motion.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Cumberland Hill

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. A down hand sink can cost you an inspection, so we work around your hours and carry commercial faucets and basket strainers. Grease is the usual enemy. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Cumberland Hill

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. It usually means worn O-rings on a swivel spout or a failed base gasket, and on some faucets it means the body has cracked and the whole faucet needs replacing. Every faucet type rebuilds differently. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Whole-house low pressure is another call. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Most sinks do not need replacing. A new tailpiece or pop-up assembly costs a fraction of a new sink and installs in an hour, and the bowl and faucet you already like stay right where they are. Finishes get matched when the part exists. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.
