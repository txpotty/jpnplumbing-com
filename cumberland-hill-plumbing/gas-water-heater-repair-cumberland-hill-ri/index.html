    Flammable Vapor Sensor Repair in Cumberland Hill

    Since about 2003, most residential gas tanks have been built with a flammable vapor ignition resistant combustion chamber. When lint, dust or pet hair coats that screen, the burner starves, the chamber overheats and a thermal switch shuts the whole heater down, sometimes several times a week. The screen and the chamber both get cleaned. A tripped thermal switch gets replaced.

    Gas Water Heater Noise Diagnosis in Cumberland Hill

    Rumbling or popping from the bottom of the tank is water boiling under a layer of sediment. Hard water and years without a flush both make it worse. We drain the tank through the bottom valve, stir up the settled scale with short bursts of cold water and keep going until it runs clear, which on a neglected tank can take a while. Whistling points somewhere else. A relief valve or a half-closed supply valve is the usual cause.

    Gas Valve and Thermostat Repair in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Lukewarm showers and a tank that never catches up often trace back to the gas control. The tech checks the dial, the immersion sensor and the outlet pressure with a manometer.     Burner Cleaning and Repair in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Yellow flame tips, soot on the burner door or a roaring sound at startup all mean the burner is not getting clean air. We pull the burner, brush the ports and blow out the orifice.     Burner Assembly and Venting Repair in Cumberland Hill
