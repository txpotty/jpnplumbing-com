    Cracked Toilet Replacement in Cumberland Hill

    Porcelain cracks below the waterline are not safely repairable, and epoxy fixes on a tank under constant water pressure rarely last more than a few months. It needs to be replaced. The cracked unit comes out, and we look at the floor under it before setting the new one, since a slow crack leak often soaks the subfloor first.

    Choosing a Toilet Brand in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield all make solid toilets, and each line has a few models we steer people away from because of weak flushes or hard-to-find parts. We will tell you which ones. What fits your bathroom comes down to rough-in and bowl shape, and we check both before you buy.

    Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Cumberland Hill

    Chair height toilets put the seat about 17 to 19 inches off the floor, roughly two inches higher than a standard bowl, and that makes a real difference for knees and hips. The drain stays the same. We often pair it with an elongated bowl and a soft-close seat, and the swap takes the same couple of hours as any other.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Late-night failures happen, usually a tank that splits along a seam or a bowl that cracks at the base and keeps seeping no matter how many towels go down. Turn off the angle stop and the leak stops. A replacement can usually go in first thing the next morning, and we will tell you if the floor needs to dry before we set the new one.

    Gravity-Flush Toilet Replacement in Cumberland Hill

    Gravity-flush toilets are the simplest and most forgiving, and a well-made one with a 3 inch flush valve will outflush most of the older toilets it replaces. Parts are easy to find. If the floor is uneven, we shim it level, then caulk the base and test several full flushes.

    Water-Saving Toilet Upgrades in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Older toilets are one of the largest indoor water users in a house, and replacing a 3.5 gallon model with a 1.28 gallon one cuts toilet water use by more than half. Dual-flush toilets add a lighter flush for liquids. Our recommendations lean on high MaP scores so the savings do not come with double flushing.

    Wall-Hung Toilet Replacement in Cumberland Hill

    Wall-hung toilets mount to a steel carrier inside the wall, so replacing one means checking the carrier bolts, the gasket on the outlet and the condition of the wall finish around it. The carrier itself sometimes needs new bolts or a coupling. We match the new bowl to the existing carrier whenever possible, which avoids opening the wall.

    One-Piece Toilet Replacement in Cumberland Hill, RI

    One-piece toilets have no tank-to-bowl joint, so there are fewer leak points and fewer crevices to clean. They are heavier and pricier than two-piece models. A one-piece toilet usually takes two people to carry upstairs, and we set it without dragging it across your tile so the floor stays unscratched and the wax ring lands centered on the flange.
