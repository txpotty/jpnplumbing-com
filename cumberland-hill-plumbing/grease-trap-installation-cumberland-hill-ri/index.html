    What Goes Into a Grease Trap Installation in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Cumberland Hill

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. We pipe the air intake the way the manufacturer and the code both require. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. We also set the unit level within a quarter inch, since a tilted trap short-circuits the baffles and loses capacity.

    Hydromechanical Grease Trap Installation in Cumberland Hill

    Flow rating is the number that matters on a hydromechanical trap, and it has to match the fastest-draining fixture combination in your kitchen. Put a 50 GPM unit on a 75 GPM load and grease goes out the back at every rush. We do the math on paper. Each sink compartment's volume, the drain size and a one or two minute drain time give us the GPM figure the code uses.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in Cumberland Hill

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. They cost more up front. Plan for a dedicated circuit near the unit, which your electrician will need to run. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.

    Grease Trap Replacement and Upgrades in Cumberland Hill

    Plastic and steel traps both wear out, just in different ways. With steel, rust eats the seams and the welded baffles first. Plastic cracks. We remove the old unit, set the new one in the same spot when it fits, and use no-hub couplings so future work is easier.

    Zurn Grease Trap Installation in Cumberland Hill

    Zurn's indoor grease interceptors cover the common sizes from small under-sink units up to 100 GPM, and we set enough of them to know where the gaskets give trouble. The lid gasket is the usual leak point after a few years. We keep spares on the truck. We mount the supplied flow control on the inlet, at the height the instructions list, and vent it.

    Commercial Kitchen Grease Trap Installs in Cumberland Hill

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in Cumberland Hill, RI

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. They fit indoors, under a sink or in a floor pit. The flow control is not optional.
