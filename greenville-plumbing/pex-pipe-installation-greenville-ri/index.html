    Why Choose JPN Plumbing for PEX Pipe Installation in Greenville, RI?

    Manufacturer rules for PEX are specific: supports about every 32 inches on horizontal runs, a minimum bend radius, and no long sun exposure. We follow them on every run.     PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Greenville

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
