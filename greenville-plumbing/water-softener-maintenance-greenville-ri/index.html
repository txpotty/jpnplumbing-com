    Standard visits typically cost $125 to $250. Rebuilding the control valve usually adds $100 to $250. Resin replacement is the big-ticket item at $300 to $700, which is still well under the price of a new system.

    Generally, servicing an existing softener does not require a permit. Replacing the whole system or changing the plumbing around it can, based on what the local building department requires, and we handle that paperwork when it applies.

    Once a year works for most homes. Every six months makes sense for very hard water or a heavily used older unit, especially with a tankless heater downstream. Between visits, check the salt level monthly and watch for spots on glassware.
