    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Greenville

    Pipe bursting means splitting the old line in place while the new one follows behind it. It works best on straight runs. A cone-shaped head breaks the old pipe outward, and a length of HDPE trails behind it in the same path. You end up with a fresh line and two small pits, and the lawn, sidewalk and driveway in between stay where they are, which is the whole reason people pick this method over a trench.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Greenville, RI

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Repeated leaks on galvanized pipe call for a new line. Age matters too, since most service lines past 40 years old are living on borrowed time. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. You see the cut-out section yourself.

    Galvanized Pipe Water Line Repair in Greenville, RI
