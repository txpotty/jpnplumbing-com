    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Greenville, RI

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. Three-wire pumps have a control box with a start capacitor and relay, and those parts fail far more often than the motor itself. Those get tested first. Swapping a capacitor costs a fraction of pulling a pump.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Greenville, RI

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A DC pump and a controller run off a few panels during daylight. You store water, not electricity. We size panels to your daily gallons.

    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Greenville

    Franklin SubDrive and Grundfos SQE are the two constant pressure systems we install most. Both use a small 2 to 20 gallon tank instead of a big one, since the drive keeps the pump running at a matched speed. The soft start is easier on the motor. Expect to pay more up front.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Greenville, RI

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They are pricier than a standard pump. The SQE pairs with a CU 301 controller for constant pressure.

    What a Well Pump Job Costs in Greenville, RI

    A well pump job usually runs a few thousand dollars, and the pump itself is often less than half of that. Labor and hoist time, along with new wire and pipe, make up the rest. You see every line item.

    Sandy Wells and Submersible Pumps in Greenville

    Sand kills submersibles early. A well that pumps sand will wear impellers down in a few years, so we check for grit in the old pump and talk about setting the new one higher off the bottom or adding a sand separator. Pump setting depth matters.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Greenville

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. Depending on depth and pump weight, it gets replaced with schedule 120 PVC or poly. The wire gets checked too. Cracked insulation and taped splices get cut out.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Greenville, RI

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. Right-sizing the pump or adding a variable-speed drive often trims the electric bill. The math does not always work. We will show you the payback.
