    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Greenville

    Sump pumps and ejector pumps seem similar at first but do very different jobs. Sump pumps push groundwater out to the yard, ejectors push sewage to the sewer, and only the ejector pit must be sealed and vented against gas. They are not interchangeable.

    Ejector Pump Alarms and Backup Options in Greenville

    A high water alarm should be on any ejector basin, since a pump failure can lead to a sewage backup quickly. Audible alarms on their own power or Wi-Fi alarms that text you give warning while the pit is still filling, not after waste reaches the floor.
