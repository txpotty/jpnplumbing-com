    Propane Water Heater Installation in Greenville

    Propane homes need heaters set up for propane, not natural gas. Propane runs at a different pressure and needs a smaller burner orifice, so we install propane-specific models or convert units with the manufacturer kit where the manual allows it. We call the propane company when needed. Every joint is leak tested.

    High-Efficiency Gas Water Heater Installation in Greenville, RI

    High-efficiency gas heaters use condensing technology to pull extra heat out of the exhaust. Condensing units vent through PVC and need a condensate line, so placement and install work differ from a standard tank. Rebates may apply. We install Rheem, Bradford White and AO Smith models.

    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Greenville

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. Because the combustion is sealed, they work well in small closets where room air is limited. Most run without electricity. We install the vent kit through an exterior wall.

    Bradford White Gas Water Heater Installation in Greenville

    Bradford White sells only through plumbers, and we install a lot of them. We install their Defender models to the manual, keeping the bottom air intake clear and the clearances right. Thick steel, good parts. Code-approved installs every time.

    Gas Line Upgrades for Water Heaters in Greenville, RI

    Moving up to a bigger or tankless gas heater can outgrow the existing gas line. We size the line from total load and run length so every appliance gets enough gas. CSST gets bonded. New lines are pressure tested.

    Proper Sizing and Venting in Greenville, RI

    Sizing and venting decide whether a gas heater runs safe and efficient. We size the heater from your peak use, then match the vent to the manufacturer table and code. Undersized vents spill CO. Draft gets tested on every job.
