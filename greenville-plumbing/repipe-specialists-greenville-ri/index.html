    PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Greenville, RI

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. For a typical house, copper often runs 20 to 40 percent above PEX, though some buyers and homeowners like the look and feel of metal pipe and are willing to pay for it. Both prices include permits, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Greenville

    Old galvanized lines shed rust flakes that clog aerators, faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. That is often the first sign. Replacing galvanized with PEX or copper ends the rust, brings pressure back and stops the leaks that start at the threaded joints where galvanized is thinnest. We disconnect and abandon the old pipe where removing it would mean tearing out finished walls.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Greenville, RI

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. We replace it with PEX or copper. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Greenville, RI

    Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a frequent sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. For homes with hard water, we usually recommend PEX-A, which resists scale and does not corrode, and we can add a softener loop or a whole-house filter during the repipe. Adding the loop now costs less than later.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Greenville

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. We use Uponor or similar PEX-A with cold expansion fittings that open the pipe end and let it shrink back over the fitting for a full-bore joint. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Two or three days covers most houses.

    Drain Line Repiping in Greenville

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.
