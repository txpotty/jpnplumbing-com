    Gas water heater installation in East Providence usually runs $1,200 to $3,000. Unit size, tank versus tankless and any gas line or vent changes the job needs all decide where yours lands in that range. Tankless costs more. You get a written quote after a visit.

    Short hot water, rusty water, rumbling or popping and water at the base are the usual signs. Units past 10 to 12 years old are good candidates for replacement even if they seem fine, since the tank lining wears out from the inside. An inspection tells you.

    Flush the tank once a year and check the anode rod every few years. Sediment over the burner makes the tank run hot and noisy, and a spent anode lets the steel rust from the inside. We can do the service.
