    Frozen Water Line Diagnosis in East Providence, RI

    Finding the frozen spot is the hard part. A thermal camera speeds it up. Fixture checks plus a thermal scan usually narrow the ice to a few feet of pipe.

    Attic and Exterior Wall Pipe Repair in East Providence

    Many homes run supply lines through attics or crawlspaces to reach bathrooms and kitchens. These runs freeze first. After repairing a split, we look at whether the line can be moved into a heated space, buried under attic insulation on the warm side, or wrapped with heat cable, since the same pipe will freeze again otherwise.

    Heat Cable for Exposed Pipes in East Providence

    Some pipes cannot be rerouted or insulated enough on their own, like a supply line under a manufactured home or a pipe through an unheated porch. Heat cable handles those. Old-style constant-wattage heat tape can overheat and is a known fire risk, so we install self-regulating cable listed for the pipe material and replace worn or brittle heat tape we find.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in East Providence

    Drain lines can freeze too, usually where a line runs through an unheated crawlspace or an exterior wall. You will see the backup first. Hot water through the cleanout thaws it, and then we check for a sag that holds water.
