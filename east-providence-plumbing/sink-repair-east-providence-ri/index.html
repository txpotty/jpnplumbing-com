    Common Sink Repairs in East Providence, RI

    Hair Clog Clearing in East Providence

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. First the stopper comes out and gets cleaned, then a hand auger goes through the trap and wall arm, with no chemicals. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in East Providence

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down the drain all day, and the trap behind the cabinet door may not get looked at until it overflows onto the carpet. We clear it and add a strainer. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in East Providence, RI

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in East Providence

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. We fix the part that is wet. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    Burst Supply Line Repair in East Providence

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Then lay towels down. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in East Providence

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in East Providence, RI

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We run a camera or a longer cable into the wall arm and look for a sagging line or buildup where the sink ties into the tub drain. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in East Providence, RI

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten. Replace the sink only if the bowl is cracked, the glaze is chipped through, or the drain has rusted into the bowl. When it is close, you get both prices. Some people repair now and remodel next year.
