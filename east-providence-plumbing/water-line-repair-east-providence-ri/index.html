    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in East Providence

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes and remodels around East Providence typically use copper or PEX supply lines, which are easier to maintain. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. You get a price on every option.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in East Providence

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, and if it reads high we replace the pressure reducing valve with a Watts or Zurn Wilkins unit. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.
