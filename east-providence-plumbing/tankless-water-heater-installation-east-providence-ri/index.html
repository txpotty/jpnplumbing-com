    Upgrading from Tank to Tankless in East Providence, RI

    Switching from a tank takes more than unbolting one and hanging the other. A 40-gallon tank burns about 40,000 BTU, but a whole-home tankless unit may need 150,000 to 199,000, so the meter line nearly always gets bigger. The vent changes as well. We plan both on the quote.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in East Providence

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.

    Tankless Water Heater Relocation in East Providence

    Moving the heater is common on a tankless job because the old tank spot is not always the best place. A garage or outside wall near the gas meter can shorten the gas run and simplify venting, but it may lengthen the hot water run to the bathrooms. Both distances matter. Long hot runs may call for recirculation.

    Residential Tankless Water Heater Installation in East Providence
