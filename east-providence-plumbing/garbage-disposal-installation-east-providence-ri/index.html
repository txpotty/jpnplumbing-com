    Disposal Upgrades in East Providence, RI

    Stepping up from an old 1/3 HP unit to a quieter model is a common request. We disconnect and remove the old disposal, check the trap and drain arm for corrosion, and install the new unit with a new mount, so the upgrade does not inherit the old unit's worn parts. Corroded drain arms get swapped. Galvanized arms rust from the inside.

    InSinkErator Model Selection in East Providence, RI

    Choosing between Badger and Evolution is where we start based on how you cook, how much noise bothers you and how much cabinet room you have. Badger units are compact and simple, while Evolution units are taller and heavier, with more grind stages and better sound insulation. The cabinet usually settles it. We measure before you buy.

    Disposals in Remodels and New Builds in East Providence

    On new builds and remodels in East Providence, we work with your contractor so the disposal, the sink and the dishwasher are planned together. That usually means a dedicated circuit for the disposal, a drain stub set low enough for the trap and a dishwasher hose path that allows a proper high loop or air gap. Planning early saves rework.

    Batch-Feed Units for Kitchens Without a Switch in East Providence

    Batch-feed disposals only run when the stopper lid is in place, which adds safety for families with kids. Because they need no wall switch, they suit kitchens where a new switch would mean cutting drywall or a tile backsplash. The drain hookup is the usual setup.

    Disposal Installation for Double Sinks in East Providence, RI

    On a double sink, the disposal goes on one bowl and a continuous waste or end outlet tee on the other. The layout keeps both bowls draining and stops the disposal from pushing water into the second bowl, a problem when the tee is too close to the discharge. Heavy-wall fittings last longer.

    Disposal Installation on an Existing Sink in East Providence

    For a common 1/2 or 3/4 HP disposal, we remove the old unit if there is one and install the new one on the existing sink. We connect it to the drain, set the P-trap and wire it, then check the clearance under the sink, which can be tight in older East Providence homes with shallow cabinets or a water filter mounted on the back wall. Cramped cabinets take some planning. Sometimes a compact model is the better fit.

    Replacing an Old Disposal in East Providence, RI

    The old unit usually drops free with a turn of the mount ring, once the power is off and the dishwasher hose and discharge tube are disconnected. If the new unit is the same brand and mount style, the swap is faster, but we still replace the flange gasket and putty when the old seal has hardened. Seals harden with age. We haul the old unit away.

    Dishwasher Connection With Disposal Installation in East Providence

    Every new disposal has a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. We knock it out with a screwdriver and hammer, fish the plug out of the chamber and connect the hose with a new clamp. Forgetting the plug floods the dishwasher. We also install an air gap or high loop, so dirty sink water cannot flow back into the dishwasher while the disposal is draining.
