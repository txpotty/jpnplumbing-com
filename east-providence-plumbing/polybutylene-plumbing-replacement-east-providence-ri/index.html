    Why Polybutylene Fails in East Providence

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a common sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. Heat makes it worse. Hot lines and fittings near the water heater fail more than anything else.

    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in East Providence, RI
