    Wall Crack Sealing for Basements in East Providence

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. We set ports along the crack, seal the face with epoxy paste, and pump in polyurethane or epoxy from the bottom port up until resin shows at the next port. Epoxy adds strength. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Clogged Drain Tile Repair in East Providence

    Footing drains clog with silt and roots over the years, and once they stop flowing the water table at the wall rises. We camera the tile from the sump inlet to see where it is blocked.     Emergency Basement Flooding Response in East Providence
