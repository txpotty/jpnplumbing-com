    Whole-House Drain Jetting in East Providence

    Slow drains in several rooms point to buildup in the main or a branch line. We jet the main from the cleanout and the branches from roof vents or from fixture access points as needed for that layout. Plan on two to three hours.

    Commercial and Restaurant Hydrojetting in East Providence

    Hotels, apartment complexes and shopping centers rely on drains that handle heavy daily use. Downtime costs them money. We set up jetting plans for each building's needs, schedule work during slow hours, and keep the camera footage on file so property managers can show tenants or owners the condition of the lines.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in East Providence

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. A line with heavy grease might need jetting every six months, while a sewer with a few root entry points might only need it every 18 to 24 months, and the footage tells us which.

    Kitchen Grease Line Jetting in East Providence

    Kitchen drains collect grease that cools and hardens along the pipe wall, especially in long horizontal runs under a slab. Cabling only cuts a narrow channel. Jetting with a spinning nozzle strips the grease from the full circumference so it flushes downstream instead of re-forming a plug at the next fitting.

    Root Removal Jetting in East Providence

    Shade trees and shrubs send roots toward small cracks in clay and cast iron pipe. Root-cutting nozzles clear them. Jetting does not stop roots from returning, because the crack is still there, but it clears the pipe completely and lets us see on camera whether a spot repair or a liner would stop the regrowth.

    Hydrojetting for Shops and Offices in East Providence, RI

    Retail and office buildings have long horizontal drain runs that collect buildup. Low-slope lines clog more. After-hours jetting keeps equipment out of hallways while tenants are working.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in East Providence, RI

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. By the time it reaches the interceptor, a lot of it has already coated the pipe. We jet the line from the kitchen to the interceptor in short passes, which keeps loosened grease from plugging the pipe farther down, and we coordinate with your pump-out company. Quarterly works for most.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in East Providence

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. A root-cutting nozzle spins through the mass. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.
