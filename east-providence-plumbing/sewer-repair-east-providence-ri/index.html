    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in East Providence, RI

    Trenchless repair means we fix the pipe without opening a trench along its full length. Two access holes, sometimes one. Either way, the driveway and the trees stay put. We tell you which one your pipe can take.

    Commercial Sewer Repair in East Providence

    Multifamily buildings and offices put several units' worth of flow through one lateral. Small defects turn into big backups.     Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in East Providence
