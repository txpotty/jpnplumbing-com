    Grease Trap Replacement and Upgrades in East Providence

    Plastic and steel traps both wear out, just in different ways. Steel rusts at the seams and the baffle welds. Plastic cracks.     Gravity Grease Trap Installation in East Providence

    A gravity interceptor works on time, not machinery. The tank holds water for roughly half an hour, which gives grease time to rise and food solids time to drop. We size by fixture count and retention time, then match the tank to what the local pretreatment office for your building will accept. No moving parts to fail.

    In-Ground Gravity Interceptor Installation in East Providence, RI

    Buffets, grocery delis and kitchens doing several hundred covers a night usually end up with an in-ground gravity interceptor of 1,000 gallons or more.     Hydro-Mechanical Grease Trap Installation in East Providence, RI

    Hydromechanical traps use baffles and a vented flow control to pull grease out of water moving at a set rate, usually 20 to 100 gallons per minute. You can put one under a sink, beside a dish station or in a pit in the slab. The flow control is not optional. Skip it and a full sink drain overruns the rating, which sends grease right past the baffles. We look for units certified to PDI G-101 or ASME A112.14.3.

    Grease Trap Installation Cost in East Providence, RI

    Price follows size and location more than brand. The low end is a small indoor unit with easy access and a short pipe run. The high end is an outdoor gravity interceptor with excavation, a traffic-rated lid and a permit, which can run five figures once concrete restoration is counted. We quote after a site visit.

    Automatic Grease Removal Units in East Providence

    An automatic grease removal unit heats the trapped grease and skims it into a container on a timer, so staff empty a bucket instead of scraping a tank. They cost more up front. Plan for a dedicated circuit near the unit, which your electrician will need to run. Fry-heavy kitchens like them most.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in East Providence

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. The air intake has to be vented to code, not left open in the kitchen. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. We also set the unit level within a quarter inch, since a tilted trap short-circuits the baffles and loses capacity.

    Grease Trap Sizing and Placement in East Providence, RI

    Sizing starts with fixture drain rates and ends with where the trap can physically go. Long runs to the trap let grease cool and coat the line, so we keep the trap as close to the fixtures as we can. Closer is better. We also check that a pump truck can reach an outdoor tank with about 100 feet of hose, since a tank the hauler cannot reach costs you more on every service.
