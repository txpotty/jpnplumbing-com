    Tree Root Sewer Clogs in Providence

    Clay and older cast iron sewer pipe have a joint every few feet. Roots love those joints. Roots from trees and shrubs push into the gaps and fill the pipe, then we cut them out and camera the line for damage. Ongoing root problems may call for a treatment schedule or lining.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Providence, RI

    Jetting sends water at thousands of psi through a nozzle that sprays backward and drives itself down the pipe. It scrubs the whole wall. A cable punches a hole through a grease plug, while a jetter removes the grease, scale and fine roots coating the pipe so the next clog has nothing to start on. We camera the pipe first. Badly cracked pipe gets a gentler approach.

    Grease and Wipes Sewer Blockages in Providence, RI

    Flushable wipes do not break down the way toilet paper does. They stay intact for weeks. Mixed with grease that hardened in the main, they make a plug that a cable can only partly clear. Jetting scours the walls and flushes the debris downstream. Toss wipes in the trash.
