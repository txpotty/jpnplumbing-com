    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Providence, RI

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. The injector and screen get cleaned or replaced, and the brine line gets checked.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Providence, RI

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A salt bridge has formed and stopped the salt from dissolving. We break up bridges, clean out mushy salt, inspect the float and brine well and repair the safety valve. Pellet salt bridges less in warm or humid spaces, so we may suggest switching if your softener sits in a hot garage.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Providence

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. This is common with older systems on treated city water. JPN Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. The softener works like new.

    Softener Repair vs Replacement Cost in Providence, RI

    Most softener repairs cost $150 to $450, while a resin replacement often runs $400 to $900 depending on tank size. A new quality softener installed typically costs $1,500 to $3,500. With a good tank and valve, repair is the better value. An obsolete valve with scarce parts tips the decision toward a new head or a new unit.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Providence, RI

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. JPN Plumbing repairs these units in Providence, RI. Media lasts a few years.
