    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Providence, RI

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A cartridge plus a same-brand trim kit can refresh an old faucet for far less than a new valve. We offer that option first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Tub Faucets in Older Homes in Providence

    Older homes hide odd tub plumbing. We find galvanized supplies that rust shut, valves soldered to copper with no stops anywhere, and spouts threaded onto steel nipples that snap when you try to turn them. We bring transition fittings. Replacing a short run of galvanized is common.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Providence, RI

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Many have separate heat and flow controls. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Same-Day Tub Faucet Replacement in Providence, RI

    Some faucet failures cannot wait. Snapped handles and broken spouts get same-day visits when a plumber is free. Close the stops or main while you wait. Leave the tub drain open.

    Standard Bathtub Faucet Replacement in Providence

    Single-handle and three-handle tub faucets come out differently. A single-handle valve often takes new trim and a cartridge from the front, while a three-handle valve usually has to be cut out of the wall and replaced with a modern pressure-balance body. The valve gets identified before we price it. Two-handle models fall in between.

    Moen Tub Faucet Replacement in Providence

    Moen Posi-Temp valves use the 1222 cartridge. A worn 1222 causes drips or weak hot water, and replacing it takes about an hour with a puller. Moentrol valves use a different part. We carry both.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Providence

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Mixing brands rarely works. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Providence, RI

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. A new valve makes sense for discontinued parts, a cracked body or an old faucet with no scald protection. We quote both options. Most people choose repair first.
