    Gas Leak Detection in Providence

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose.     Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Providence, RI

    A kitchen remodel or a new tankless water heater can push a gas system past what its original pipe can deliver. Tankless units need a lot of gas. Many tankless heaters are rated at 150,000 to 199,000 BTU, which can be more than double what an old tank heater used, so the line feeding it often has to be upsized back toward the meter. Load calculation comes before any pipe. The new pipe gets sized so every burner in the house still has full pressure at once.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Providence

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Coastal areas have higher water tables, which affect basement drainage, sewer lines and septic system design. We replace corroded sections with polyethylene pipe laid with tracer wire, using an approved transition riser where the line comes up out of the ground at the house. Exposed sections get coated steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Providence building officials.
