    Constant Pressure Pump Systems in Providence

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Providence, RI

    No-water calls often end at the control box, not the pump. Three-wire pumps have a control box with a start capacitor and relay, and those parts fail far more often than the motor itself. Those get tested first. A $60 part beats a $2,500 pump pull.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Providence

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. We replace it with schedule 120 PVC or poly pipe, depending on the depth and the pump weight. Wire gets the same look. Cracked insulation and taped splices get cut out.

    Well Pump Sizing in Providence

    Sizing starts with the well log. The log, or a sounder down the casing, gives us static water level and well depth, and a draw-down test tells us roughly how many gallons per minute the well can actually give. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Jet Pump Suction Line Repair in Providence, RI

    Foot valves and suction lines cause most jet pump trouble, not the pump. A pinhole in the suction pipe lets air in, the pump loses prime, and it runs dry until the seal burns up. The suction line gets a pressure test. A well that is too deep for suction needs a submersible, and we will quote that instead.

    Well Pump Efficiency Upgrades in Providence, RI

    Older 1 HP pumps running against a 60 psi switch use more power than most people expect, especially on a well with a long horizontal run to the house. A correctly sized pump, or a variable-speed drive, can cut the run time. Not always worth it. We will show you the payback.
