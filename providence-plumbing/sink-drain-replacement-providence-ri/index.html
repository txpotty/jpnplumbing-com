    Vessel and Grid Drain Replacement in Providence

    Vessel sinks use a different drain. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted grid or umbrella drain, or water leaks out the slots. We see the wrong drain often. Swapping it takes under an hour.

    Commercial Lavatory Drain Replacement in Providence

    Public restroom drains take constant use.     Leaking Drain Assembly Replacement in Providence

    Water on the cabinet floor needs tracking, not guessing. After drying everything we fill and drain the bowl and watch each joint with a dry towel. Supply leaks look similar. We check those too.

    Code Updates During Drain Replacement in Providence

    Old drains sometimes break current rules. S-traps and drum traps were common long ago, and replacement brings them up to a vented P-trap. That can mean an air admittance valve. Local rules decide.

    Choosing Drain Material in Providence, RI

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It does not rust, it stands up to drain cleaners, and it costs less than brass, while polished 17-gauge brass makes sense under a pedestal sink or any trap you can see. Some houses use black ABS instead. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    P-Trap Replacement in Providence

    Thin chrome traps are the ones we replace most. They rust inside and their nuts seize, so we cut them out and fit heavy-wall PVC or polished brass. Then it gets a full flow test. Total time is often under an hour.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Providence

    Traps leak at the joints first. Slip nut washers flatten and harden with age, and a trap opened often for clogs usually has stripped threads. A new trap kit costs little. Labor is most of the bill.
