    Common Sink Repairs in Providence, RI

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Providence

    A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons a year. On compression faucets the fix is a new washer and seat, while cartridge and ceramic disc faucets need the cartridge or its seals replaced. We carry Moen, Delta, Pfister and Kohler cartridges. Odd brands take a day to order.

    Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Providence

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Low Water Pressure at the Sink in Providence, RI

    Unscrew the aerator before anything else. A clogged aerator screen causes most low flow at a single faucet, and if cleaning it does not help we check the cartridge and the angle stop for debris. Low pressure everywhere points to a different problem. We test the pressure regulator in that case.

    Garbage Disposal Repair in Providence

    A disposal that hums but will not spin is usually jammed. From below we turn the flywheel with a 1/4 inch hex key, hit the reset button and look for a bent impeller. Leaks are a different story. A cracked housing means a new disposal, usually an InSinkErator or a Waste King.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Providence, RI

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. Kitchen clogs are grease and food that cooled and stuck, and bathroom clogs are hair wound around the stopper with soap scum holding it together. We clear it with a hand auger. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Providence

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Providence, RI

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. A leak like that is usually the overflow gasket or the flange putty, which only get wet when the bowl fills. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.

    Commercial Sink Repair in Providence

    Restaurants and salons cannot wait a week for a sink. Three-compartment sinks and hand sinks have to work for the health inspector, so we schedule around your hours and bring commercial-grade faucets and basket strainers. Grease is the usual enemy. We also check the grease interceptor connection when the drain backs up.

    Finding Sink Repair Near Me in Providence, RI

    A local plumber knows the houses. Older neighborhoods tend to have galvanized stubs and chrome traps, while newer subdivisions have PEX and plastic traps with compression stops, so we bring parts for both. That cuts out a supply-house run.
