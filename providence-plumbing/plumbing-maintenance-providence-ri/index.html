    Hidden Leak Checks in Providence, RI

    A leak you cannot see still costs money. We shut off every fixture and watch the meter's leak indicator, then use acoustic and thermal tools to track hidden leaks in walls, under slabs and in buried lines. Rhode Island has a large share of older housing, including multifamily homes with galvanized supply lines and cast iron drains. Catching it early limits damage. Usually the fix is minor.

    Sewer and Drain Camera Inspection in Providence, RI

    A camera shows what the sewer looks like inside. Roots, sags, cracks and grease show up on camera, especially in old clay and cast iron. You get a copy of the video. If cleaning or repair makes sense, we explain the choices.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Providence

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast.
