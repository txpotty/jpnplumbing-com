    Residential Plumbing Service in Providence, RI: What We Fix

    Drain Cleaning and Main Line Clogs in Providence

    Slow drains in more than one fixture point to the main line. One slow sink by itself is usually just the trap or a short branch line, but when the tub gurgles as the washer drains, the blockage is further out and needs a machine cable or a jetter run from the cleanout. Older clay and cast iron lines often have roots.

    Faucet and Fixture Repair in Providence

    Most dripping faucets need a cartridge, not a new faucet. The truck has cartridges for the big brands, Moen and Delta included, and when you do want new fixtures we install them, connect new braided supply lines, and check the angle stops under the sink while we are there. Old stops fail when touched.

    Water Heater Repair and Replacement in Providence, RI

    Thermocouples, gas valves, elements and relief valves are the common water heater repairs. We service Rheem, Bradford White, Navien and Rinnai units, and when a tank is leaking from the shell we replace it with proper venting, an expansion tank and a drain pan, usually the same day. We haul the old tank away.

    Pipe Repair and Repiping in Providence, RI

    Pinhole leaks and rust-colored water from the tap usually point to supply pipe near the end of its working life in an older house. We patch single leaks with a copper repair or PEX transition. More than one leak usually means repiping. Freeze splits get the same treatment, cut out and replaced with new pipe rated for the job.

    Faucet, Toilet and Shower Valve Repair in Providence, RI

    Hard water chews through faucet cartridges and toilet fill valves. We rebuild shower valves, replace stems and cartridges and fix leaking tub spouts, and if a fixture is too corroded to save, we install the replacement and haul the old one away so it is not sitting in your garage. A slow drip still adds up on the bill.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Providence, RI

    Water stains on a ceiling rarely sit right under the leak. Basements are standard in most homes, and many rely on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet seasons. Our techs use acoustic sensors and thermal cameras, then isolate lines with pressure tests, which lets us open one small hole in the right spot instead of cutting drywall all over the house. The repair usually happens the same visit.

    Toilet Repair and Replacement in Providence

    A running toilet can waste a couple hundred gallons a day. We replace flappers, fill valves and wax rings, and when the bowl is cracked or the toilet is older than the last bathroom remodel, we set a new water-saving model and level it on the flange. A toilet that rocks gets shimmed so the wax seal holds.

    Drain Cleaning and Sewer Camera Service in Providence

    Wipes are not flushable, whatever the package says. Most slow drains come down to grease, hair or wipes, and for a main line backup we cable it open, then run a camera to look for roots, offset joints or a belly where water sits in the pipe. We send you the video file.
