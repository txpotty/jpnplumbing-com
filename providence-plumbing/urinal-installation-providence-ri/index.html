    Rough-In Plumbing for Urinals in Providence, RI

    Rough-in decides whether the finish goes smoothly. We set the carrier, run a 2 inch waste line to the outlet height on the spec sheet, stub the 3/4 inch supply where the flush valve will land, and tie the vent in before anyone hangs drywall. The rough gets inspected before cover. Then the walls close.

    ADA Urinal Installation in Providence

    Accessible urinals start at the carrier. We set the carrier so the finished rim lands at 17 inches or lower, keep the clear floor space free of partitions and door swings, and mount the flush handle or sensor where a seated user can reach it. We measure the rim twice. Inspectors measure too.

    Waterless Urinal Upgrades in Providence

    Switching to waterless is mostly a fixture swap. We remove the flush valve, cap the supply and set the waterless bowl on the same carrier. Some codes want the supply left in place. That allows a future change back.

    Drainage and Venting for Urinals in Providence, RI

    Urinal drains clog with scale, not paper. Urine leaves a hard mineral buildup inside the trap and the drain line, especially on waterless units and low-flow fixtures that send less water through, so we slope the line properly and add a cleanout where we can. A proper vent keeps odor out. We tie in within code distance.

    ADA-Compliant Urinal Installation in Providence

    At least one urinal in a public restroom with more than one usually has to be accessible. That means a rim no higher than 17 inches above the floor, an elongated bowl projecting at least 13-1/2 inches from the wall, and a 30 by 48 inch clear floor space in front. Flush controls must be reachable. We mark it all during rough-in.

    Flushometer Urinal Installation in Providence

    Manual flushometers are still the most common setup. Manual valves go on a 3/4 inch line with the control stop tuned and the vacuum breaker at the correct height. Handle side follows the layout. ADA units need the handle within reach.

    Old Urinal Removal and Replacement in Providence

    Old urinals come off harder than they went on. Corroded hanger bolts and a flush valve seized to the spud mean we come prepared to cut, and we protect the wall tile while we do it so the new fixture does not sit against broken edges. We never reuse the old gasket. The flange gets cleaned to bare metal.

    Residential Urinal Plumbing in Providence, RI

    Home urinals use the same rules as commercial ones. The drain is usually 2 inch, the trap is built into the china, the fixture needs a vent within code distance, and the flush valve needs either a 3/4 inch supply or a lower-flow residential valve on 1/2 inch. Permit rules apply too. We check that first.
