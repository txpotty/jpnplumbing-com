    Picking a Tankless for Your Providence, RI Home

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Providence

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Shut the gas and cold isolation valve now. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Providence

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. A photo of the error code helps a lot. A leaking heat exchanger means replacement, usually done same day or next day.

    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Providence

    Whole-house electric tankless performance drops when the incoming water is cold, since the unit has to raise the temperature further at the same amperage. Stiebel Eltron and EcoSmart are the common brands. A heat pump water heater is sometimes the better fit, and we will tell you.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Providence, RI

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. Both are in our wheelhouse.

    Navien Venting and Install in Providence

    Navien allows 2-inch or 3-inch PVC venting on its condensing units, with maximum lengths that drop as you add elbows. Our vent runs stay within spec. Go past them and the warranty suffers.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Providence

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. They run on a single 20 or 30 amp circuit and work well for a far bathroom or an office sink. Most take under two hours.

    Commercial Tankless Replacement in Providence

    Cascade controls on a commercial rack decide which unit fires first, and after one unit is replaced they need rebalancing. Otherwise one unit does all the work. Descaling gets scheduled by usage.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Providence, RI

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. Expect a bigger gas line, a new vent run and a wall-mounted spot for the heater. The old flue gets capped.
