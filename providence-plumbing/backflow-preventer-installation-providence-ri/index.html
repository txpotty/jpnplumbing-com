    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Providence, RI

    Pressure vacuum breakers are the low-cost answer for lawn irrigation in Providence, RI. A spring-loaded air inlet opens when the pressure falls, which breaks the siphon, but they only protect against back-siphonage, so they must sit above every downstream outlet and cannot be used where back pressure is possible.     Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Providence

    A double check valve assembly, or DCVA, uses two spring-loaded checks in series with four test cocks. It is the usual pick for low-hazard lines such as many fire sprinklers, and because it does not discharge water, it can go in a vault where local rules allow it, sized so it does not rob your system of pressure in Providence. Test cocks stay reachable.
