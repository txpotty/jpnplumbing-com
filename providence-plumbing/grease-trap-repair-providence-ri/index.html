    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Providence

    Grease Trap Repair vs Replacement Cost in Providence, RI

    Age and material decide most repair versus replace calls. Once a steel unit has rust holes in the body, it is finished.     Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Providence, RI

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Over the years, hydrogen sulfide gas softens the concrete above the water line. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Nobody goes in without a gas monitor, a tripod and a second person topside.

    Preventative Grease Trap Maintenance & Repair in Providence, RI

    Most trap repairs we do trace back to pumping intervals that stretched too long. The common rule is to pump when grease and solids fill a quarter of the liquid depth. After a repair, we measure the grease cap and solids with a sludge judge and suggest a pumping schedule based on how fast your trap actually fills. A kitchen running two fryers can hit that mark in a couple of weeks.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Providence, RI

    A wet floor around an under-sink trap usually points to a failed lid gasket or a loose inlet coupling. Older steel bodies rust out along the bottom seams, too. We dry the area, fill the trap and watch where the water shows up. Sometimes it is a $15 gasket. An opened seam is another story, and we will say up front if a patch will not hold.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Providence, RI

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. The inlet tee sends flow down below the grease layer. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. We replace broken tees in Schedule 40 PVC and set the drop to the depth the code or the manufacturer calls for.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Providence

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. When that wall spalls, grease slips through to the second compartment. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.
