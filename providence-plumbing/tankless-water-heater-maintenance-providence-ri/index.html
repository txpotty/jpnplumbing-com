    Yearly Tankless Inspection in Providence

    A yearly inspection costs less than one service call for a dead heater. Gas connections get a leak test, and we work the pressure relief valve. We pull the air filter and check the igniter.     Home Tankless Flushing in Providence

    Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a frequent sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. Inside a tankless heater, that water runs through a small copper or stainless coil while the burner fires, and every minute of hot water leaves a little calcium behind. A pump on the service valves pushes descaling solution through the coil for roughly an hour. The flow returns. The temperature holds steady and the high-limit shutdowns stop.

    Navien Condensing Tune-Ups in Providence

    Navien NPE units are condensing heaters with a second heat exchanger that pulls extra heat out of the exhaust before it leaves the house. That second exchanger makes acidic condensate. We clear the condensate trap and check the neutralizer media. On A-series models with the built-in recirculation pump, we test the pump and the buffer tank too.

    Tankless Banks for Restaurants and Apartments in Providence

    Cascade systems link several tankless units to one controller that fires them in turn. They see heavy use, and scale builds faster than at home. Each heater gets descaled, and we check that the controller spreads the load evenly. Quarterly service is common.

    Step-by-Step Tankless Flushing in Providence

    First we shut off the gas and power, then close both isolation valves so the heater is cut off from the house plumbing. Solution goes from a bucket into the cold port, out the hot port and back to the bucket. Once the cycle ends, we rinse with clean water and pull the inlet screen. Then we check for leaks.

    Maintenance for Every Tankless Brand in Providence, RI

    We service Bosch, Rheem, Takagi and Navien heaters around Providence, and each one has its own filter locations and code list in the manual. The manufacturer's steps come first, not a generic checklist. That matters for warranty claims down the road.

    Rinnai Tankless Service in Providence

    Rinnai units tell you a lot through the controller. A code 11 is a failed ignition, while code 12 is a flame that lit and then dropped out. We clean the Rinnai air intake filter and look over the flame rod for soot or cracks. The LC codes are scale warnings, and they usually clear once the exchanger has been flushed and the unit is reset.

    Service Valve Kit Installs in Providence, RI

    Plenty of tankless heaters went in without service valves, and without them there is no clean way to hook up a descaling pump. A pair of isolation valves with purge ports goes on the hot and cold lines. Most kits include a relief valve port as well. After that, every flush takes minutes to set up.
