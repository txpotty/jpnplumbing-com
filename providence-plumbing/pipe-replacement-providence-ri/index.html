    Repiping and Pipe Replacement Options in Providence, RI

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Providence, RI

    Copper still has fans. It handles heat well and lasts decades. We install Type L copper for most interior supply lines, sweat or press the joints, and support every run with hangers at the spacing code requires, so the new pipe does not rattle or sag. Costs more than PEX.

    Whole-Home Repiping in Providence

    Repiping sounds bigger than it is. Most homes stay livable during the job. We work room by room, restore water most evenings, and keep dust down with plastic sheeting and drop cloths, then patch every opening before we leave. Clean finish.

    Corroded Pipe Replacement in Providence, RI

    Corrosion shows up as green or white crust on copper, rust on steel, or pinholes that drip. Water chemistry plays a role. We replace corroded sections or the whole system depending on how widespread the damage is, including fittings near the heater. Look at the joints.

    Emergency Pipe Replacement in Providence, RI

    A burst pipe needs fast action. Close the main shutoff right away. Then call, because water spreading through walls and floors does damage every minute, and we can usually get there, stop the leak and replace the failed section the same day. Drying comes next.

    Commercial Pipe Replacement in Providence

    Commercial buildings often have larger supply lines and more fixtures. Downtime costs money. We plan pipe replacement in phases whenever the layout allows it, keeping restrooms and kitchens running while we work on other sections. Night work available.

    PEX Repiping in Providence

    PEX installs quicker. Flexible lines snake through walls with fewer openings, and home-run systems from a manifold let you shut off individual fixtures without killing water to the house. Popular choice.

    Polybutylene Pipe Replacement in Providence

    Polybutylene is gray or blue plastic installed from roughly the late 1970s to mid 1990s. It degrades from the inside. Failures tend to be sudden splits at fittings rather than slow drips, and some insurers refuse to cover homes that still have it. Replace it.

    Galvanized Pipe Removal in Providence, RI

    We often leave old galvanized pipe in the wall. It is disconnected and capped, while new lines run beside it, which keeps drywall damage low. Only accessible pipe comes out.
