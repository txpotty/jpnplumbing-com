    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Cranston

    Cost depends on whether it is a clog or a construction job. Clearing a fixture or main line is a few hundred dollars at most. Jetting and sewer repairs cost more, and yard drains are priced by length and digging. You get a written quote first.

    Simple downspout extensions are a reasonable DIY project. French drains are harder. They need proper slope and a legal outlet, and digging without an 811 locate is risky.

    Installations like French drains, dry wells and sewer repairs carry a workmanship warranty. Drain clearing has a separate guarantee. If a drain we installed does not perform as promised, we fix it at no labor cost.

    Most homes never need scheduled drain cleaning. A slow drain is the signal. Homes with root-prone sewers, old cast iron, or a kitchen that sends a lot of grease down the drain do better with a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the line slows to a crawl.

    Some do. Busy kitchens benefit from jetting once a year. Sewer laterals with a history of roots need cutting every 12 to 18 months, and older cast iron with heavy scale may need jetting every couple of years to keep it from narrowing back down to a trickle.

    Watch for water pooling near the house after storms. Mulch washing onto the walk is another sign. Inside, a damp crawlspace, a musty basement or efflorescence on the foundation wall all mean water is reaching the house instead of being carried away, and the problem usually starts at a downspout or a low spot.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It is quick and cheap. Jetting scours the full pipe wall, lasting longer, but it costs more and needs sound pipe.

    A typical French drain or downspout system takes one to three days. Larger projects run longer. Dry wells, long trenches or saw-cut concrete can push it toward a week.
