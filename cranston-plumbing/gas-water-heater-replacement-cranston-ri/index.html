    Propane Gas Water Heater Replacement in Cranston, RI

    Propane heaters run at about 11 inches of water column instead of the 7 inches typical for natural gas, so the gas valve and orifice have to match the fuel. A natural gas unit does not go on propane unless the manufacturer allows a conversion. We confirm pressure with a manometer.

    Standard Gas Water Heater Replacement in Cranston

    A standard 40 or 50 gallon atmospheric tank is what most homes have, and swapping one like for like is a two to three hour job when the venting and gas line are in good shape.     Choosing Between Gas Tank Brands in Cranston, RI
