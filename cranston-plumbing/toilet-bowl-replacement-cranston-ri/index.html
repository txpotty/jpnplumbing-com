    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Cranston, RI

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. That is the moment to check it. We fix a cracked or sunken flange before the new bowl is set, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.

    Toilet Bowl Replacement for Older Homes in Cranston, RI

    Houses built before the 1970s often have a lead closet bend or a cast iron flange caulked with oakum, and those need gentle handling when the bowl comes off. Old pipe cracks easily. Prying on the flange is something we avoid and use repair rings or flange adapters made for cast iron, so the drain stays intact.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Cranston

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Many are not sold separately. If the pair is unavailable, a full replacement is the clean answer, and we will price both.

    Emergency Bowl Replacement in Cranston

    Shut the angle stop behind the toilet the moment you see water coming from a cracked bowl, then pull towels around the base to slow the spread. Then call us.     Chipped or Stained Bowl Replacement in Cranston, RI

    Deep chips in the glaze collect bacteria, and hard water rings that have etched the surface will not come out with any cleaner. Replacing the bowl solves both problems.
