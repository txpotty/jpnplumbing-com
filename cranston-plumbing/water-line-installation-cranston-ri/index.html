    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Line Installation in Cranston

    Residential water lines usually run $1,800 to $5,500 depending on length, soil and material. Long runs through rock, trenchless boring or copper pipe can push it to $6,000 to $10,000.

    A typical residential line takes one to two days, plus time for locates and inspection. Rocky ground or a long run can add a day or two.

    Trenching disturbs a narrow strip, about a shovel width wide. We backfill, compact and regrade when we finish, and if the route crosses concrete or mature planting we will talk through trenchless options first.

    Our water line installs carry a warranty on parts and labor. You see the warranty terms on your estimate, and the pipe manufacturer adds its own long coverage, which on PEX and HDPE often runs decades when the pipe is installed to spec. Your records stay on file with us.
