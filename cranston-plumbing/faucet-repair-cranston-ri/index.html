    Common Faucet Repairs in Cranston, RI

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Cranston

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Cranston

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. Frozen pipes are a regular winter risk in Rhode Island, especially in exterior walls, crawlspaces and unheated areas of older homes. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Cranston

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. Hoses split near the quick-connect. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Low Water Pressure Faucet Fix in Cranston, RI

    Weak flow at one faucet usually points to a clogged aerator or a cartridge packed with scale, not a problem with the house pressure. Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a common sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. The aerator comes off with a key and gets cleaned or replaced, then check the cartridge screens. If every fixture is weak, the problem is elsewhere.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Cranston

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Dripping Faucet Solutions in Cranston

    Every drip has a source, and on a faucet that has dripped for years there is usually more than one worn part. Anything worn gets replaced, and the rest gets checked. Each valve type, from compression to ceramic disc, wears in its own way, so the repair is matched to the valve inside yours.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Cranston

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Try lifting out the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Cranston, RI

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. We measure the pipe stub and bring the right style, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.
