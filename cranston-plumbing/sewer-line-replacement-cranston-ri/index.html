    Sewer Line Replacement Options in Cranston, RI

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Cranston

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. When the camera stops at a pile of broken clay or a flattened section of Orangeburg, the only fix is to dig down, clear the debris and lay new pipe through that stretch. Being in the trench shows us what the surrounding soil is doing. Often there is a void or a root mat right at the failure point.

    Spot Sewer Repairs in Cranston, RI

    Sometimes one joint is the whole problem. If the camera shows a single offset or a cracked section and the rest of the line is round and clean, we dig one small hole at the marked depth and replace that piece with PVC and shielded couplings. Far cheaper than a full run. We only suggest it when the footage backs it up.

    Cured-in-Place Sewer Lining in Cranston

    Lining builds a new pipe inside the old one. After cutting roots and jetting the line, we pull in a resin-filled liner and inflate it until it cures against the old pipe wall. Collapsed pipe will not work. Lining shines under finished basements and slabs. You will get a straight answer on whether yours is a candidate.

    Emergency Sewer Replacement in Cranston, RI

    Sewage coming up a floor drain is an emergency. The first visit gets a camera in the line so we know if it has caved in or only needs clearing to buy time. If it has collapsed, we start permit and locate requests that day. JPN Plumbing can often run a temporary bypass. That keeps one toilet and the kitchen sink usable while the dig gets scheduled.

    Sewer Permits and Code in Cranston, RI

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. We file it, schedule the inspection and meet the inspector on site, so you never have to take a morning off to stand in the yard. Code covers pipe size, fall, bedding and cleanout placement. Unpermitted sewer work can show up on a home inspection when you sell.

    Pipe Bursting Under Driveways in Cranston, RI

    Concrete is expensive to replace. With pipe bursting, the new line goes in under the driveway or tree from one pit to another, following the old pipe's route. The HDPE is butt-fused on site into one long piece. Most bursts finish in a day. You end up patching two small pits rather than a whole slab.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Cranston

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. The crew trenches the full length of the lateral, removes the failed pipe and sets new PVC on compacted bedding with steady fall. Where we can, the sod is cut and set aside before the digging starts. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.

    Upgrading Old Sewer Pipe Materials in Cranston

    Clay, cast iron and Orangeburg all fail in their own ways. Roots get into clay joints, cast iron rusts out along the invert, and Orangeburg fiber pipe squashes flat over the years. Modern plastic pipe avoids all three problems. HDPE is fused into one continuous piece with no joints for roots to find.
