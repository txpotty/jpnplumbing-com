    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Cranston

    Household sewers clog slowly. Grease, hair and wipes build up over months. Each flush adds a little more to the pile at a rough joint or a low spot, until the 4 inch line is down to a trickle and the lowest drain in the house starts to back up, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Cranston

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. Cabling just opens a hole. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Sewer Camera Inspections in Cranston, RI

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.

    Scheduled Sewer Cleaning in Cranston, RI

    History predicts the next backup. Repeat lines get a schedule. Homes with past root problems, older cast iron or a lot of kitchen grease do well on a cleaning every 12 to 24 months, timed before the growth or buildup closes the line.

    Emergency Sewer Cleaning in Cranston

    Sewage on the floor cannot wait. We take emergency calls at night. A tech arrives with a main line machine, protective gear and a wet vac, ready to clear roots or a collapsed section and stop the backup before it spreads into more rooms.

    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Cranston

    No cleanout means pulling a toilet. That adds time. A two-way cleanout by the foundation gives us access toward the house and toward the street, so future service is quicker, and the work is done to local code with any required permit.

    Commercial Sewer Cleaning in Cranston, RI

    Commercial lines take a beating. Kitchens put grease into the line daily. Offices and retail buildings deal more with paper towels and foreign objects flushed in public restrooms, which snag on joints and catch everything after them, and our heavy jetters handle 6 inch commercial pipe without much trouble.

    Clay and Orangeburg Sewer Lines in Cranston

    Older properties may still have clay or Orangeburg pipe. Each fails in its own way. Roots love clay joints, while Orangeburg fiber pipe tends to squash into an oval and eventually collapse, so we clean these lines carefully and map the joints and soft spots on camera.
