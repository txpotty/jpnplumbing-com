    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Cranston

    Two independent checks and a relief valve make up an RPZ. When pressure between the checks gets too close to supply pressure, the relief opens, dumping water to the floor or a drain so nothing can pass upstream, which is why we never bury one in a pit in Cranston. We install Watts and Febco units.

    Pool and Spa Fill Backflow Protection in Cranston, RI

    Pool auto-fill lines and hose connections give chlorinated water a straight path back to the supply. A hose bibb vacuum breaker costs a few dollars.
