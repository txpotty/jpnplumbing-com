    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Sump Pump Installation in Cranston

    Figure on roughly $800 to $2,000 for most basement sump pump installs in Cranston. The low end covers a pump swap in an existing pit, while a new pit cut through the slab, a long discharge run, or a battery backup pushes the total toward the top of that range. You get a written price after we look.

    Changing out a pump in an existing pit takes about two to three hours. Plan on most of a day for a new pit with a slab cut and patch, since the concrete needs a little time to firm up before the pump goes into service.

    A straight pump replacement generally goes without a permit. New pits, new discharge routes and new circuits may, so we check the local rules and pull permits when they apply.

    Most standard installs finish within 4 to 8 hours. Long discharge runs or a new pit can add several hours, and if we need a new circuit from the panel, an electrician may need to come out the same day, which can push the job into the evening. We give a timeline at the visit.

    Submersibles sit in the pit, sealed, and run quiet. Pedestal pumps keep the motor up on a column above the water, which makes them cheaper and easier to service but louder, so they work best in an unfinished utility room rather than a finished family room. Both move water fine.

    Discharge belongs in the yard, a dry well, or an approved drainage area at least 10 feet from the foundation. Tying a sump pump into the sanitary sewer is not allowed in most places, and it can draw a fine.

    Watch for constant running, rapid cycling or grinding noises. Rust stains and a float that sticks are warning signs too, as is water sitting above the switch level without the pump starting. Replace it before the next storm.

    A primary pump does the everyday work. A secondary pump, often on a battery, waits in the same pit, and it kicks on if the primary fails, loses power, or gets overwhelmed by more water than it can move during a heavy storm. Two pumps, two check valves.
