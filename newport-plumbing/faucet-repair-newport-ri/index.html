    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Newport, RI

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately.     Emergency Faucet Repair in Newport

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Turn the two small valves under the sink clockwise. If they will not turn, use the main shutoff for the house. We come fast for these.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Newport, RI

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. Cracked or discontinued faucets are different. We give you both prices so the choice is yours.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Newport, RI

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Newport, RI

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. We can check whether yours qualifies. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard use their own cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Newport

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Newport, RI

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. Swapping a common cartridge like the Moen 1225 takes about half an hour, cleanup included.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Newport, RI

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touchless faucets add a solenoid and a battery pack to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.
