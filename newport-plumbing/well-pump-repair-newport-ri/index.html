    Control Box and Surge Repair in Newport, RI

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Sump pumps work hard during storms, and a battery backup keeps them running when the power goes out. Surges from lightning are the usual cause of control box failures. We test capacitors, relays and overload protection, replace what failed and recommend a surge arrestor at the box if one is missing. Arrestors are cheap.

    No-Water Emergency Repair in Newport

    A dead pump leaves the house without toilets, showers or drinking water. JPN Plumbing answers emergency well pump calls throughout Newport and nearby areas and puts them ahead of routine work. The technician checks power and the switch first. Those two items fix a surprising share of no-water calls without anyone having to touch the pump hanging down in the well.

    Grundfos Well Pump Repair in Newport

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Most Grundfos calls involve a sensor or a drive fault. JPN Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Newport, RI well. Electronics fail less often than people think.

    Pressure Switch Replacement in Newport

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. This is among the least expensive well repairs.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Service in Newport, RI

    Franklin Electric motors and control boxes are in a lot of local wells. We work on Franklin motors, QD control boxes and SubDrive units regularly. Common Franklin parts are on the truck. A motor that tests bad on a megohm meter usually gets replaced rather than rewound, since a new motor costs less than shipping and rebuilding the old one.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Newport, RI

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. JPN Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and watches it reach cut-out pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.

    Same-Day Well Pump Repair in Newport

    Hot weather makes a dry house worse. No-water calls get a fast response and, when parts allow, a same-day fix. If a new pump has to be ordered, we can sometimes set up a temporary solution to get the house through. While the truck is on the way, the dispatcher can help you check the breaker and switch.

    Well Pump Repair vs Replacement Cost in Newport, RI

    Switch and control repairs often cost $150 to $500, while a pressure tank replacement typically runs $600 to $1,500. A submersible pull and replacement runs about $1,800 to $5,000 and up, based on depth and horsepower. Replacing an aging pump during a pull saves paying for a second pull later. We lay out both options.
