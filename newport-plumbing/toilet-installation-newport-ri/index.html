    Wall-Mounted Toilet Installation in Newport

    Wall-mounted toilets hang off a steel carrier bolted to the floor inside the wall. An access plate hides the tank. Because the carrier carries all the weight, we anchor its feet to the slab or framing first and check it for plumb before the drywall or tile goes up. Plan on a remodel, not a swap.

    Low-Flow WaterSense Toilet Installation in Newport, RI

    WaterSense toilets are capped at 1.28 gallons per flush, and some dual-flush models drop to around 0.8 for liquid waste. That adds up fast. A family of four swapping one old 3.5 gallon toilet can save thousands of gallons a year, and some water providers offer rebates worth checking. Pick a strong MaP rating.

    Smart Toilet Installation in Newport, RI

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Newport

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Newport, RI

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. A flushometer needs a 1 inch supply and steady pressure. Before setting fixtures in an office or shop, we check that the building supply can deliver the flow the valve is rated for, often 20 to 25 gallons per minute at the fixture. Fixture counts follow code.

    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Newport

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. Below about 20 psi, they flush poorly. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Newport, RI

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. That leaves the old flange sitting low. Either an extender ring brings the flange up or a new flange goes in, and relocating a toilet on a slab means saw-cutting concrete to move the drain. Moving it on a wood floor is easier.

    Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Newport

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. Water comes from a T-valve at the fill line. Powered seats with warm water and a dryer need a GFCI outlet within a few feet, and if one is not there, we line up a licensed electrician before we mount anything. Non-electric seats take about thirty minutes.
