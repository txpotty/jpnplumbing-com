    Type L Copper Repiping in Newport

    Copper has been plumbing homes for generations, and done right it lasts a long time. We use Type L, not the thinner Type M, and we support it with the right hangers so it does not rub or rattle in the walls. All joints use lead-free solder. Copper can pinhole in aggressive water, so we ask about your water and any past leaks before recommending it over PEX.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Newport

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Rocky glacial soil and ledge are common in Rhode Island, which can make digging for sewer and water lines slower and costlier. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    Drain Line Repiping in Newport

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. We camera the lines first. Failing sections are replaced with PVC or ABS sloped and vented to code, which drains faster and resists buildup much better than the pipe it replaces.

    Drywall Patching and Restoration in Newport, RI

    Repiping needs small openings in walls and ceilings to reach fixtures and pull pipe. We cut clean, square openings, protect the area around them and patch every hole once the plumbing passes its test. Textures get matched. We match knockdown, orange peel or smooth walls, so the patches are ready to prime and paint.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Newport, RI

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Flexible PEX soaks up some of the pressure spike behind water hammer, and its long fitting-free runs leave fewer weak points. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    Kitec and Polybutylene Replacement in Newport

    Kitec, an orange or blue PEX-AL-PEX pipe with brass fittings, and gray polybutylene are both known for failures. Both still turn up in older homes. The pipe at the water heater usually tells us which one you have, and we replace the full system so the home is easier to insure and to sell.
