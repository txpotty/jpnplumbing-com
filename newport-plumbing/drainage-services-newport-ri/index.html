    Frequently Asked Questions About Drainage Services in Newport

    Most basic drain clearing in Newport, RI runs $150 to $500. Main lines cost more than sinks. Jetting costs more than cabling, and sewer repairs run from a few thousand for a spot fix to much more for replacement.

    A single fixture drain usually takes about an hour. Plan on one to two hours for a main line. Add another 30 to 60 minutes for a camera inspection, and more time if the cleanout is buried, missing, or the line has to be accessed by pulling a toilet.

    We advise against it. Caustic cleaners can soften old PVC joints and eat at cast iron and metal traps. When they fail, the trap stays full of caustic liquid that the plumber has to deal with carefully.

    Yes, drain clearing comes with a service guarantee for a set number of days. Repairs and installs get a workmanship warranty. The terms depend on what the camera shows, since a line with roots growing through a cracked joint will clog again no matter who clears it, and we tell you that up front instead of after the callback.

    Call when more than one drain is slow, or when a toilet gurgles while the washer drains. Those point to the main line. Outside, call when water stands in the yard for more than a day after rain, when a downspout overflows the gutter in every storm, or when water shows up in the crawlspace or basement after a heavy rain.

    Watch for water pooling near the house after storms. Washed-out mulch tells you too. A damp crawlspace or white crust on the foundation means water is reaching the house.

    Roots usually enter through an existing crack or joint, then widen it as they grow. Over years, that can split clay pipe. A root mass can also push a joint out of line, creating an offset that catches waste, and in bad cases the pipe collapses and has to be dug up or lined from the inside.

    Drain cleaning usually means a cable machine with a cutting head that bores through the clog. It works fast and costs less. Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the whole pipe wall clean of grease, scale and roots, so it lasts longer but costs more and needs a pipe that is in good enough shape to take the pressure.
