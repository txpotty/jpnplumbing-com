    What Goes Into a Sewer Replacement in Newport, RI

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Newport

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. A hydraulic rig at the receiving pit pulls the bursting head through the old line, splitting it into the soil, while a continuous length of fused HDPE follows behind which leaves a single joint-free pipe in the ground. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.

    Foundation Protection during Sewer Replacement in Newport, RI

    Rocky glacial soil and ledge are common in Rhode Island, which can make digging for sewer and water lines slower and more involved. A sewer leaking under or beside the house keeps the soil wet in one spot, and that uneven moisture can push or settle part of a slab. When we replace a line near the foundation we dig carefully, avoid undermining footings, and compact the backfill in lifts. Slow work near the house.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Newport, RI

    Bursting and pulling replace the pipe. CIPP lining coats the inside of the existing pipe instead. We use bursting when the old line is broken or undersized, because it leaves you with brand new pipe rather than a liner inside an old one, and lining when the pipe is still round and mostly intact. Each has its place.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Newport, RI

    Trenching lets us fix grade. That is its big advantage. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, while a trench lets us lay new pipe at the right pitch. Bellies disappear.

    Under-Slab Sewer Replacement in Newport

    Sewer pipe under a slab is the hardest kind to reach. There are two options. We can cut and remove a strip of floor inside the house and patch it after, or dig a tunnel beneath the foundation from the yard, and the right choice depends on the length of the run, the flooring, and what sits above the pipe.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Newport

    Digging is still the right method for a lot of lines. If the old pipe has caved in, trenchless has nothing to follow. Sharp bends, a belly that needs regrading, or a route that should change all point toward an open trench, and we leave the yard as close to how we found it as dirt allows. Grass grows back.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Newport

    Commercial laterals are often 6-inch pipe and carry grease or heavier flows. There is often a grease trap or interceptor to work around. We schedule the work around your hours, set up temporary bypass pumping when the building needs to stay open, and work with the utility on the main connection. Weekend starts are common.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Newport, RI

    Pipe bursting needs two pits. One sits at the house, the other at the far end of the run. A cone-shaped head is pulled through the old line, breaking it outward, while fused HDPE pipe follows right behind it, which puts new pipe in the same spot without digging the yard. Driveways stay put.
