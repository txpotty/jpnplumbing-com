    Water Softener Replacement & Upgrades in Newport, RI

    Upgrading an old timer-based softener is one of the easiest ways to cut salt use. Modern units meter water use, send salt reminders by app and come in larger capacities for growing families. The savings add up. We swap it out in a few hours.

    Water Hardness Testing and Programming in Newport

    Testing is what makes the settings right. We test for hardness, iron and chlorine before installing, then program the regeneration frequency and salt dose so you get soft water without wasting salt. Iron changes the math. We review the settings with you before we leave.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Newport, RI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. JPN Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Water Softener Installation for New Construction in Newport, RI

    Building a home in Newport, RI? Adding a loop during framing is easy and cheap, since the plumber can route the main through the right spot before drywall goes up. Builders call us in early. We keep outdoor taps off the softener.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Newport

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. We set it up for you.

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Newport

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. Filters get changed on schedule.

    Culligan Water Softener Installation in Newport

    Culligan systems are common in homes around here, and we install and connect them. Each install follows the brand's specs for drain, bypass and power, then we program it to your water test. Connections and setup included. Existing loops make it faster.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Newport

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. That pays back over time. We install several models.
