    Why Choose JPN Plumbing for Grease Trap Cleaning in Newport, RI?

    Skipped pump-outs usually announce themselves at the floor drain first, with gray water pooling around the grate after the dish machine drains. Then comes the smell. That sour, rotten odor is grease breaking down and releasing hydrogen sulfide gas. Hydrogen sulfide also eats concrete, so a neglected interceptor wears out years early.

    • Odor Control, A pumped trap stops the sour, rotten-egg smell that decaying grease gives off.
    • Prevent Blockages, Regular pumping keeps floor drains flowing during the dinner rush.
    • Extended Lifespan, Less hydrogen sulfide in the tank means slower corrosion of concrete and baffles.

    Citywide Coverage

    School cafeterias and church kitchens around Newport usually have small traps that get forgotten for a year at a time. Those go on a schedule as well. For a kitchen that serves lunch on weekdays and shuts down for summer, pumping once a quarter is usually plenty.

    Experienced Team

    Every tech on a JPN Plumbing truck is trained on confined-space rules before they touch a large interceptor. Nobody goes in a tank without a gas monitor. Hydrogen sulfide builds up in grease tanks, and at high levels it can knock a person down in a couple of breaths, so the monitor stays on.

    Grease trap waste goes to a permitted receiving facility, never down a manhole or a storm drain. Every load gets logged. That log links the gallons from your trap to the facility that took them, the same trail inspectors look for.

    Scheduled Grease Trap Maintenance Plans in Newport

    Most pretreatment programs use the 25 percent rule: pump before floating grease and settled solids together take up a quarter of the trap's liquid depth.     Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Newport

    A trap that stinks between pump-outs usually has a flattened lid gasket or a vent problem. Both get checked every visit. Gaskets are cheap to swap, but a broken vent can push sewer gas into the kitchen and slow the drain too, so it is worth chasing. Odors are often fixed the same day.

    Grease Trap Cleaning Cost in Newport, RI

    Cost follows trap size and fill level. A 20-gallon under-sink trap is a short visit. A 1,500-gallon interceptor left five months needs a truck, a pressure wand and disposal fees by the gallon, which puts it at the top of the range. A steady schedule lowers what each visit costs.

    Grease Line Jetting in Newport

    Grease that slips past a full trap coats the 4-inch line out to the main. The buildup forms rings that are thickest along the bottom. Snaking just bores a small path that closes up in weeks, but a rotating jetter nozzle strips grease off the full wall of the pipe. We run a camera after to confirm.

    Keeping Grease Trap Costs Down in Newport, RI
