    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Newport

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. Water that seeps from the arm connection runs back along the pipe into the wall, and you only find out when the paint on the other side starts to bubble. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Newport, RI

    Tub and shower combos share one valve, one drain and a diverter between them. That means a worn diverter steals pressure from the head, a clogged tub drain slows the shower and a leaky valve shows up at both the spout and the head. We check all of it. The overflow gasket gets a look too, since it is a common hidden leak.

    Leaking Shower Cartridge Repair in Newport

    Cartridges wear from the inside. Grit and scale scratch the sealing surfaces, the O-rings flatten and harden, and eventually the valve cannot fully stop the water when the handle is closed. We swap in a new one. We use genuine parts and coat the new one with silicone grease so the handle turns smoothly.

    Clogged Shower Drain Clearing in Newport, RI

    Hair and soap scum cause nearly every slow shower drain. We pull the strainer and run a hand auger through the trap, and if the clog sits past the trap in the branch line, we switch to a drum machine with a smaller cable. Drain cleaner usually misses the clog. A hair catcher over the strainer keeps the next one from forming.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Newport

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. The fix is in the valve. Leaks at the arm threads are different, and those get new thread tape and a snug turn.

    Shower Diverter Repair in Newport, RI

    Diverters send water up to the head instead of out the tub spout. When the gate in a lift-style spout wears or a three-handle diverter stem loses its washer, water keeps pouring from the spout and the shower gets weak. Usually a new spout fixes it. Spout replacements take about twenty minutes.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Newport, RI

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. The Moen 1222 runs the Posi-Temp line, and the Delta RP46074 runs the Monitor 13 and 14 series, and both wear out on a schedule you can almost set a calendar by. Both ride on our trucks. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.
