    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Newport

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. As it moves, it splits the old clay or cast iron and shoves the pieces into the surrounding soil while a string of new HDPE pipe follows right behind it. Fused joints, no gaps. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.

    Cast Iron Sewer Line Lining in Newport, RI

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, lining beats breaking up the floor in two rooms.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Line Work in Newport, RI

    Restaurants feel a sewer failure fast, because grease and a dish machine put heavy load on a 4- or 6-inch building drain all day. Lining can be scheduled overnight or on a day you are closed. A trench through a parking lot means lost spaces and a blocked entrance for a week or more. Liners skip that.

    Root Intrusion Fixes in Newport, RI

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once in, they fill the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.

    Perma-Liner makes inversion drums and epoxy systems for lining 2- to 6-inch drain lines, which covers most of what we see in houses.
