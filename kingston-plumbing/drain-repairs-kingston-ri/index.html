    Leaking Drain Pipe Repair in Kingston, RI

    Drain leaks are low pressure, so they drip instead of spray. Most go unnoticed for months. Corrosion, shifting framing and cracked fittings are the usual causes, and we trace the leak by running each fixture and watching the joints, then replace the bad length with pipe that matches what is already there. Every repair is done to code.

    Recurring Clog Repair in Kingston

    Monthly clogs mean there is a physical cause. Find it and fix it. The usual suspects are a trap arm with too little slope, a branch line with a belly, scale built up inside old metal pipe or a fitting with a rough edge that snags paper, and most of them are obvious once the camera or a pulled trap shows the inside. We repair the part that catches debris.

    Outdoor Drain Repair in Kingston

    Downspout lines and yard drains fail without much warning. Water pools at the house. We clear the line, camera it for crushed or disconnected sections, and replace the damaged pipe so roof water reaches the outlet instead of soaking the soil next to your foundation. The discharge point gets checked as well.

    Drain Repair Cost Comparison in Kingston, RI

    A trap or slip-joint repair is one of the cheapest plumbing jobs. Often under an hour. Replacing a section of branch drain in an open wall or crawlspace costs more but is still a modest repair, and cutting into finished drywall or swapping an entire stack sits at the top of the range. You get every option with a number.

    Cast Iron Drain Stack Repair in Kingston, RI

    Old cast iron stacks rot from the inside out. Cracks start along the bottom. Rust scale catches debris and hairline cracks let water seep into walls and floors, so we cut out the failing section and replace it with ABS or PVC using shielded transition couplings. Full stack replacement gets a separate quote.

    Vent Pipe Repair in Kingston

    Sewer gas leaking into a room can point to a missing vent. Old renovations often skipped it. Bird nests, debris and roofing work can block existing vents, and where a vent was never installed we can add an air admittance valve in locations where local code permits it. Good venting keeps all trap seals intact.

    Tub and Shower Drain Repair in Kingston, RI

    Hair, soap scum and minerals narrow bath drains over time. Gaskets fail too. We replace worn pop-up assemblies, broken tub shoes and leaking shower drain bodies, often working from inside the tub so the ceiling underneath stays intact. When access is needed, we keep the opening small.

    Sewer Drain Line Repair in Kingston
