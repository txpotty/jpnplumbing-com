    PEX Main Water Line Installation in Kingston

    The main from the meter to the house feeds every fixture you own. A leaking old service line shows up as a soggy patch in the yard, a higher water bill and weak pressure everywhere. New PEX goes in its own trench. At the house we add a new full-port ball valve for the main shutoff.

    Manifold and Home-Run PEX Layouts in Kingston, RI

    Think of a manifold like an electrical panel, but for water lines. Each fixture gets its own line from a central header, and each line gets its own valve, so you can shut off one toilet without killing water to the kitchen. You buy more pipe. What you get back is very few hidden fittings and steadier pressure when two people shower at the same time.

    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Kingston, RI

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. Every one is listed for potable water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    Remodel and Addition PEX Rough-In in Kingston

    Rough-in heights matter more than people think. If the shower valve sits two inches low or the vanity stubs miss the cabinet, the wall gets reopened after tile, and that costs time and money. We check heights with your contractor before drilling. Then we pressure test with the walls open.

    Leak Repair with PEX in Kingston, RI

    A single bad section is not always a reason to repipe. We can cut out a split or corroded stretch of copper, transition to PEX with a listed fitting, and have the water back on in an hour or two. It is fine for an isolated leak. By the third leak, though, we will talk to you honestly about whether the rest of that copper is close behind.

    Copper to PEX Conversions in Kingston

    Pinhole leaks in copper tend to come in clusters. Aggressive water, fast flow and soil contact under slabs can pit copper from the inside, and the first few pinholes are typically followed by more. Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a common sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. Converting to PEX stops the cycle. Where new PEX meets existing copper stubs or valves, we use listed transition fittings.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Kingston

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. For tight attics we lean toward PEX-A.
