    Grease Trap Installation Cost in Kingston, RI

    Price follows size and location more than brand. The low end is a small indoor unit with easy access and a short pipe run. Outdoor tanks with digging and a permit sit at the top of the range. We quote after a site visit.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Installation in Kingston

    Outdoor gravity interceptors run from 750 gallons to well past 2,000, and setting one means an excavator on site and a traffic-rated lid if it sits in the drive lane. Before anything ships, we check that the tank and covers carry an H-20 traffic rating where trucks will roll over them. Most go in over two to four days. We set the inlet a few inches higher than the outlet and check both with a laser before any dirt goes back in.

    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Kingston

    Inside a hydromechanical interceptor, baffles do the separating while a flow control fitting with an air intake holds incoming water to the rated flow. We pipe the air intake the way the manufacturer and the code both require. Get that wrong and the trap siphons. We also set the unit level within a quarter inch, since a tilted trap short-circuits the baffles and loses capacity.

    Grease Trap Brands We Install in Kingston

    Zurn, Watts, Schier and Endura cover most of what we set in commercial kitchens, plus precast concrete tanks for the big outdoor interceptors. The lid gaskets and baffles differ from brand to brand. Parts availability matters.
