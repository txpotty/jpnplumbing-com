    What Our Gas Leak Repair Visits Cover in Kingston, RI

    Corrugated Stainless Steel Tubing Repair in Kingston, RI

    CSST is the yellow-jacketed flexible tubing used in many newer homes, and it can be damaged by a nearby lightning strike if it was never bonded. Bonding means a 6 AWG copper wire from the gas piping to the electrical grounding system. Plenty of older installs lack it. We repair CSST only with the matching brand's fittings, because TracPipe and Gastite parts do not interchange. A splice that skips that rule fails the inspection.

    Interior Gas Pipe Repair in Kingston, RI

    Inside the house, gas leaks hide at unions in the basement and at the threaded nipple behind the furnace, places nobody looks. Testing runs from the meter inward, one section at a time. Drywall stays intact when possible. Rerouting with CSST past a leak in a finished wall is often cheaper than cutting and patching drywall. Nothing is turned back on until the gauge holds steady.

    Propane Gas Leak Repair in Kingston, RI

    Propane behaves differently from natural gas because it is heavier than air, so a leak settles into basements and crawlspaces instead of rising. We check both regulators, the one at the tank and the one at the house. Low spots get sniffed first. Your propane supplier owns the tank in most cases, so we coordinate with them for anything on the tank side of the first regulator. Rural properties with underground tanks get a line test too.

    Appliance Connector Replacement in Kingston

    Old uncoated brass connectors, the kind made before the mid-1970s, are the subject of consumer safety warnings because the flare nut can separate from the tube. If your range or water heater still has one, it should come out. We see them weekly. In its place goes a coated stainless connector and a new shutoff valve, plus a drip leg if the code calls for it. Bubble testing follows before the pilot is lit.

    Commercial Gas Leak Repair in Kingston

    Commercial gas piping often runs at 2 psi or higher with regulators at each appliance bank, so a leak test there is not the same as a house test. Regulator vents and lockup pressure get checked along with every joint. Rooftop units leak too. If a shutdown would cost you a dinner rush, we valve off just the bad branch and leave the rest live. Staff get a short briefing on the shutoff locations.

    Emergency Gas Leak Repair in Kingston, RI

    Hissing at the meter, a sulfur smell in the kitchen, a dead patch of lawn or a carbon monoxide alarm all mean the same thing: get everyone outside before you touch anything. From the yard or a neighbor's porch, call the gas utility first. JPN Plumbing runs emergency gas calls in Kingston, RI around the clock. Once the utility has shut the meter, our tech isolates the leaking branch and makes the repair. Unaffected appliances usually come back the same day.

    Outdoor Gas Line Repair in Kingston

    Grills, fire pits, pool heaters and patio heaters often sit at the end of a long buried line that nobody has looked at since it went in. More outdoor lines get hit by shovels and fence augers than fail from rust. Dead grass is a tell. We pressure test the outdoor branch on its own, locate the leak and replace the run with polyethylene and a tracer wire. Above-ground stub-ups get a new riser and a shutoff valve.
