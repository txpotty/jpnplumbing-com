    Power Vent Water Heater Replacement in Kingston

    Blower motors and pressure switches are the weak points on power vent tanks. A second blower failure on an older tank is usually the point where a new heater makes more sense. We confirm a dedicated outlet first.

    No Hot Water Calls in Kingston

    No hot water at all usually points to a dead thermocouple or a gas control valve that has failed after years of service. Those get tested before anything else. On a tank past ten years, a new heater usually beats a new valve.

    Atmospheric Vent Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kingston

    Atmospheric vent heaters last 10 to 12 years on average, and the end usually shows up as rusty hot water or a slow drip from the bottom. The new tank ties into your existing gas line and flue. Closets need enough combustion air. A tank in a tight closet needs louvered doors or a ducted air supply, and we check that before lighting it.

    Emergency Gas Water Heater Replacement in Kingston, RI

    Water on the floor around the tank usually means the inner tank has rusted through, and that is not a repair. Close the cold water valve above the heater and set the gas knob to off. Then give us a call. We carry 40 and 50 gallon tanks and can usually have hot water back the same day.

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Kingston
