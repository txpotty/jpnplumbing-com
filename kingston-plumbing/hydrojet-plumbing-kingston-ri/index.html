    Hard Water Scale Removal in Kingston, RI

    Scale builds slowly inside drain pipe, narrowing it a little more each year. Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a frequent sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. A jetter with a scouring nozzle strips that scale from the walls, and on heavily scaled cast iron we may follow up with a descaling chain head to finish the job. The difference shows on camera.

    Grease Trap Line Cleaning in Kingston, RI

    Restaurant kitchen lines carry hot grease that hardens as it cools. Much of it coats the pipe before it ever reaches the interceptor. Short jetting passes keep loosened grease from plugging the line downstream. Quarterly works for most.

    Same-Day Jetting for Backups in Kingston

    Sewage in a tub or floor drain is a main line blockage. Hold off on running water. We open the line, jet out the cause, and camera it to see if a repair is needed.

    Hydrojetting vs. Snaking in Kingston, RI

    Snaking pokes a hole through a clog. Jetting cleans the whole pipe. For a single clog in an otherwise clean line, snaking is cheaper and works fine, but for a line that clogs every few months, jetting removes the buildup that keeps catching debris.

    Preventive Hydrojet Maintenance in Kingston

    Preventive jetting pays off for lines that clog more than once a year. We set the interval from camera findings. Grease-heavy lines may need six-month service, while root-prone sewers can often go 18 to 24 months.

    Tree Root Removal with Hydrojetting in Kingston

    Roots find their way into sewer lines through cracked joints, and older clay pipe has a joint every few feet. We use a spinning root-cutter nozzle. After cutting, the jet flushes the root pieces down to the main, and the camera shows which joints let the roots in so you know where a future repair or liner would go.

    After-Hours Hydrojet Service in Kingston

    Backups at night or on weekends are handled with the same gear as any weekday call. Our on-call trucks carry jetters. We can clear and jet a line in one visit instead of returning Monday.

    Hydrojetting for Cast Iron Pipes in Kingston, RI

    Older cast iron drains rust and scale from the inside, leaving a rough surface that grabs debris. Jetting can remove that buildup. Badly corroded cast iron with thin walls or cracks needs gentler settings or no jetting at all, and the camera tells us which before we start, along with whether the pipe is a candidate for lining.
