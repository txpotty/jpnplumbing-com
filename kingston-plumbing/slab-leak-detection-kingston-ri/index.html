    Slab Leaks in Older Homes in Kingston, RI

    Older homes in Kingston, RI often have soft copper or galvanized steel under the slab, and both wear out with time. Copper fails at bends and where it rubs concrete, while galvanized rusts from the inside until it splits. Our techs know where these fail. It lets us test the most likely spots first.

    Residential Slab Leak Detection in Kingston

    Single-family homes and townhouses across Kingston get the same step-by-step process. Meter test first. We isolate, trace and listen, then confirm with a thermal scan or tracer gas, and once the spot is marked you get a plain explanation and repair prices in writing.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Kingston

    Rerouting leaves the failed pipe in the slab. New pipe runs through the walls or attic instead, so there is no concrete work and the cost is often lower than going through tile. We usually use PEX because it handles heat and resists corrosion. It suits tile and hardwood homes.

    Tracer Gas Leak Detection in Kingston

    PEX and CPVC do not carry sound well. For those, we drain the line and push in a safe hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas, which rises through the slab and gets picked up by a sniffer at the floor surface, often through grout joints and hairline cracks. It catches leaks the microphone cannot in Kingston homes.

    Slab Leaks in Commercial Buildings in Kingston

    Commercial slabs are thicker and carry larger pipe, so leaks can move more water and be harder to hear from the surface with a standard ground microphone. Our approach changes for those. Our techs combine zone isolation, line tracing and tracer gas, scheduling the testing around your business hours in Kingston.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Kingston, RI

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Kingston, RI

    Hot water leaking under the slab warms the floor above it. A thermal camera shows that warm bloom through tile, vinyl and even thin carpet, often spreading outward from the leak point in a recognizable shape. It only works on hot lines. It shrinks the search area before we listen.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Kingston

    Hot lines leak under slabs more often than cold ones. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.
