    Copper Repair vs Repipe Cost in Kingston, RI

    A single copper leak repair is affordable and fast. Patterns matter. If you have had two or more pinhole leaks in a year, the copper is likely thinning throughout the system, so repiping often beats another round of spot fixes on total cost. We price both.

    Copper Pinhole Leak Repair in Kingston

    Pinholes form when the inner wall of copper pipe erodes or pits. Hot lines get it worst. They often appear in thin Type M pipe and on hot water lines where velocity and temperature are higher, so we cut out the affected section and replace it with Type L copper or a press fitting. More pinholes often follow.

    Under Slab Copper Leak Repair in Kingston, RI

    Copper lines under a slab can wear through where they rub concrete or gravel. A hot spot underfoot is common. We isolate hot and cold lines with pressure tests and locate the leak with acoustic and thermal equipment, then choose between a small slab cut at the leak and rerouting the line through the walls or attic. We quote each way.

    Preventive Copper Pipe Maintenance in Kingston, RI

    Copper gives warning signs before it leaks. They are easy to overlook. Green crust at fittings, a stain on a basement ceiling and high static pressure all point to trouble ahead, and a walkthrough of exposed copper in the basement, crawlspace or utility room catches many of them. A regulator often helps.

    Copper Leak Behind Walls in Kingston, RI

    Small pinholes can suddenly widen and spray water into a wall or ceiling. Find the main valve.     Solder Joint Leak Repair in Kingston, RI
