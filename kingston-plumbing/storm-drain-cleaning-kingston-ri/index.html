    Downspout and Roof Drain Lines in Kingston, RI

    Buried downspout lines clog with roof granules. Debris piles up fast. We jet each line and test with a hose to make sure water exits at the far end, away from the slab, and we check scupper discharge on flat roofs.

    Dry Well Cleaning and Inspection in Kingston, RI

    Dry wells soak storm water into the ground. Sediment clogs the rock. We vacuum the settling chamber, inspect the inlet and outlet and check drain-down time, since silt in the chamber and drainage rock slows how fast the well empties, and if it no longer works, we explain repair or replacement options.

    Jetting Silt From Storm Pipe in Kingston, RI

    Silt packs storm lines tight. Cables poke through it. Jetting scours the inside of the pipe and pushes silt, grease and roots toward the outlet, which restores full capacity and keeps the line clear much longer than snaking.
