    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Kingston

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? A repair coupling is often the right call.     Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Kingston, RI

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper.     Type L vs. Type K Copper in Kingston, RI

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is lighter and mostly goes indoors. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against stone contact and soil chemistry. Same fittings, different pipe.
