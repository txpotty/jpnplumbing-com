    Gas and Electric Tank Checks in Kingston, RI

    Gas and electric tanks share the same steel shell, but the parts that wear out are different. Gas units get a burner, thermocouple and vent check. Electric units get a meter test on both elements and a look at the wiring at each thermostat, where loose terminals can scorch and melt the insulation. Both get a flush.

    Yearly Maintenance Plans in Kingston, RI

    A maintenance plan puts your tank on the calendar so nobody has to remember. You get a reminder call when the flush is due.     Rental and Commercial Tank Logs in Kingston

    Landlords and property managers with a dozen units need to know which tank is next to fail. We log every tank we service.     Gas Venting Tune-Up in Kingston, RI

    Atmospheric gas tanks vent by draft alone, so the vent connector needs upward slope of at least a quarter inch per foot and tight joints with three screws each. Draft at the hood gets checked first. Lint on the flame arrestor of an FVIR tank can starve the burner and kill the pilot. We vacuum it clean.

    Standard Tank Flushing in Kingston

    Flushing is the one job every tank needs, gas or electric, new or old. Hard water makes it more urgent. We drain the tank completely, refill it partway, drain it again and repeat until the hose runs clear, which on a heavily scaled tank can take four or five cycles. Then we close the valve and check that it reseals without a drip.

    Maintenance Versus Replacement Costs in Kingston, RI

    A yearly flush costs a small fraction of a new installed tank, which often runs $1,400 to $3,000 or more depending on size and venting. You get a straight answer on which one is worth it.     Expansion Tank Service in Kingston, RI

    A thermal expansion tank is the small steel tank on the cold line above the heater, and it gives heated water somewhere to go when a check valve or pressure reducer closes the system. We test the air side with a tire gauge. The precharge needs to match static pressure in the house. A waterlogged expansion tank sounds solid when you knock on it, and it lets pressure spike every time the burner fires.

    Electric Element Check in Kingston

A failed lower element shows up as hot water that runs out halfway through a shower. We replace it with the matching wattage and voltage.
