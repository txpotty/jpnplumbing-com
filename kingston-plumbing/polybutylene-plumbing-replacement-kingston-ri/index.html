    Why Polybutylene Fails in Kingston

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. Over the years, the pipe and its acetal fittings get brittle from the inside, then crack along the inner wall until one splits under normal pressure. Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a frequent sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.
