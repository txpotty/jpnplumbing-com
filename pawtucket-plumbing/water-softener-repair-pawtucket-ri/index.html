    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Repair in Pawtucket

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. Salt sits there unused, and regeneration fails. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. Cracked tanks get replaced.

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold.     Resin Bed Problems in Pawtucket

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.
