    Repiping for Hard Water in Pawtucket, RI

    Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a common sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    Slab Leak Repiping in Pawtucket

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Rocky glacial soil and ledge are common in Rhode Island, which can make digging for sewer and water lines slower and costlier. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed. That stops the current leak and removes the chance of the next one under the same floor.

    PEX Repiping in Pawtucket, RI

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. Flow stays strong at the last shower.

    Drain Line Repiping in Pawtucket

    Drain pipe wears out too, especially old cast iron that cracks and scales up inside. Repeat clogs, sewer smells and several slow drains at once often mean the drain pipe itself is failing. A camera shows the condition. New PVC or ABS, sloped and vented properly, replaces the bad sections and drains better than the old pipe.

    Condo, Townhome and Multi-Family Repipes in Pawtucket

    Shared walls and HOA rules make repiping attached homes more involved. We coordinate with the HOA, schedule water shutoffs that may affect neighbors and protect adjoining units from dust and noise as much as we can. Property managers get phased schedules. For larger buildings, we repipe one stack or wing at a time so residents can stay in place.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Pawtucket, RI

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. Attic PEX gets kept away from flues and out of UV.
