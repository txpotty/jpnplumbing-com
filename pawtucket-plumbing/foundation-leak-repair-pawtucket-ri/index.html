    Copper Pipe Leak Repair in Pawtucket, RI

    Older homes in Pawtucket, RI often have soft copper running under the slab. Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a common sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing.     Water Line Reroute in Pawtucket

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. The floor stays whole. PEX is the usual choice since it flexes around framing and doesn't pit, and drywall openings stay small, mostly at fixture walls, for quick patching.

    Direct Foundation Leak Repair in Pawtucket, RI

    Direct repair means cutting into the floor right over the leak. It is often the cheapest fix.
