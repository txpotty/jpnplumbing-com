    Main Line Drain Cleaning in Pawtucket

    Toilets bubbling while the washer drains? That points to the main.     Bathroom Sink, Tub and Shower Drains in Pawtucket, RI

    Toothpaste and soap scum glue hair into a stringy plug. Pop-up stoppers catch it first.     Hydro Jetting for Drains in Pawtucket

    Jetting does what a cable cannot. Water at around 3,000 to 4,000 PSI scours the whole pipe wall. Cabling opens a path, but jetting strips the buildup off the walls. It works best on a kitchen or main line that keeps clogging every few months no matter how often it gets cabled.

    Restaurant and Shop Drains in Pawtucket, RI

    Commercial kitchens fight fats, oils and grease every day. The grease interceptor helps, but lines still coat. A regular jetting schedule, set by how much frying your kitchen does, keeps the line open, and we leave a service report you can show the health inspector or the landlord.

    Jetting Versus Cabling in Pawtucket, RI

    Cabling and jetting solve different problems. A cable cuts through a blockage. Jetting strips grease and scale off the walls and carries it out of the line, so we cable first on a hard root mass and jet afterward when the line has years of buildup on it.

    Main Sewer Drain Cleaning in Pawtucket

    Tub filling when the toilet flushes is the classic main line sign. Access through a cleanout saves time and money. Ours is a sectional cable with a cutting head, fed through the 3 or 4 inch line until it breaks through, then recommend a camera pass to look for a break, and if there is no cleanout at all we can add one.
