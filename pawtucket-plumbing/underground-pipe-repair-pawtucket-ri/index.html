    Shallow irrigation leaks are sometimes a fair DIY job. Service lines are different. Main water lines and sewer laterals require locating, utility marks and code-compliant fittings rated for burial, which is why a licensed plumber is the safer choice for anything carrying drinking water or sewage.     Water lines get PEX or Type K copper. Each is approved for direct bury. Sewer lines get Schedule 40 PVC, and the final choice depends on the existing pipe, the soil and what your local code allows. Couplings get sized to both pipes.
