    Orangeburg and Clay Pipe Repair in Pawtucket

    Orangeburg is bituminous fiber pipe that was laid in houses mostly from the late 1940s into the early 1970s. Over time it softens, goes oval and blisters on the inside, so a liner has nothing round to bond to and bursting is usually the better fix. Clay is different. Vitrified clay that still holds shape lines well.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Pawtucket

    Prep is most of the job. We jet the line at high pressure to strip roots, grease and loose scale, then camera it again to measure the exact footage before cutting the liner tube to length. Skip that step and the liner can wrinkle or fail to bond. Lining works best on cracked or root-intruded lines that still hold their round shape.

    Trenchless Drain Pipe Repair in Pawtucket, RI

    Smaller drain lines inside the house can be lined too, including 2-inch kitchen drains and the 3-inch cast iron stacks found in many older homes. We work through cleanouts and roof vents, so the drywall and tile stay where they are. Branch lines get reinstated with a robotic cutter. That saves opening a finished wall.

    Root Intrusion Repair in Pawtucket, RI

    Roots enter at the joints, where clay bells or cast iron hubs have a small gap or a failed gasket. Once in, they fill the pipe. We cut them out with a mechanical root cutter or a hydro jet, then line the pipe so those joints are sealed for good and the line stops needing a cleaning every few months. The roots cannot get back in.

    Sewer Line Video Inspection in Pawtucket

    We record the full camera run on every job, from the cleanout out to the city main or the septic tank. You get a copy of the video file and a written summary.     Cast Iron Sewer Line Repair in Pawtucket, RI

    Cast iron rusts from the inside out, and the bottom of the pipe goes first. Plumbers call that channel rot, a groove that eats through the bottom. We descale the pipe with chain knockers or carbide cutters spinning on a flexible shaft, which brings the wall back to bare metal so a liner can bond to it. Under a slab, that beats jackhammering.

    Commercial Trenchless Sewer Line Work in Pawtucket, RI

    Restaurants feel a sewer failure fast, because grease and a dish machine put heavy load on a 4- or 6-inch building drain all day. Lining can be scheduled overnight or on a day you are closed. A trench through a parking lot means lost spaces and a blocked entrance for a week or more. Liners skip that.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe Relining in Pawtucket, RI

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.
