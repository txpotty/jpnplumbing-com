    Granite and Quartz Composite Sinks in Pawtucket

    Composite sinks are heavier than stainless and usually thicker at the drain, so standard strainers sometimes need a longer threaded body. Strainer fit gets checked first. When the strainer is too short for the sink thickness, we replace it instead of cranking it down and chipping the drain opening.

    Undermount Sinks on Stone in Pawtucket, RI

    Stone shops vary on how they mount undermounts. Some epoxy threaded studs into the stone, others use a rail system, and we work with whichever one your countertop shop prefers. Either way, the bowl gets braced. We do not connect the trap until the adhesive has cured.

    Farmhouse Sink Installation in Pawtucket, RI

    Apron-front sinks rest on a support frame built into the cabinet, not on the countertop. We build that frame from 2x4s or a steel bracket, set the sink dead level and leave the right reveal for the stone shop. Drain placement is planned early. Many farmhouse sinks drain at the back, and the trap has to work around that.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Pawtucket, RI

    Undermounts hang from clips anchored into the underside of the stone, with silicone run around the rim. We support the bowl from below with a brace while the silicone cures, usually overnight, before loading it with water. Skipping the cure time leads to sagging. A sink that drops even an eighth of an inch opens a gap where water and crumbs collect along the counter edge.

    Sink and Disposal Installed Together in Pawtucket, RI

    A new sink is the cheapest time to add a disposal. We mount the disposal on the new flange, set the trap height for its outlet, and knock out the dishwasher plug so the hose has somewhere to go. Power is a separate question. We check for a switched outlet in the cabinet.

    Workstation Sink Installation in Pawtucket, RI

    Workstation sinks have a ledge for cutting boards, colanders and drying racks, and many are deep single bowls with the drain off center. The trap layout follows the offset drain, which lets the disposal fit and leaves storage room on one side. Faucet reach matters too.

    Stainless Steel Sink Installation in Pawtucket

    Stainless sinks come in gauges from 16 to 22, and the lower the number, the thicker the steel. Thin 22 gauge bowls flex and drum when the disposal runs, so we add sound pads or suggest an 18 gauge or heavier sink if you have not bought one yet. Top-mount and undermount plumb differently. Either way, we hook up the faucet, strainers and disposal.

    Top-mount Sink Installation in Pawtucket

    Drop-in sinks are common in Rhode Island homes with laminate or tile counters. The rim holds the sink up. We check the cutout, run a bead of silicone under the lip and tighten the mounting clips evenly around the bowl so the rim seats flat. Then the drain and faucet get connected and tested.
