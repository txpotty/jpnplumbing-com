    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Pawtucket, RI

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Pawtucket

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. We figure out which type you have first. It matters. We carry the common washers, seats and cartridges, so the fix usually happens on the first trip.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Pawtucket, RI

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long. Cartridges get matched for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Pawtucket, RI

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. Commercial cartridges are on the truck. We set metering faucets to run long enough for a full rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Kitchen Sprayer and Diverter Repair in Pawtucket, RI

    Spray heads on kitchen faucets get clogged with scale, and side sprayers lose pressure when the diverter inside the faucet body wears out. Diverters are cheap parts. Touch and touchless faucets add a solenoid and batteries to the list, and we test those before replacing anything.

    Bathroom Pop-Up Drain Repair in Pawtucket

    Pop-up drains on bathroom sinks fail at the pivot ball, the clevis strap, the lift rod or the stopper itself, and a slow drain often turns out to be hair wrapped around the stopper. Pull the stopper first. We replace the pop-up assembly in a matching finish when the old one is corroded or the pivot leaks.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Pawtucket

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. New washers go in and the seat gets dressed or replaced with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Pawtucket

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Shut the stops first. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.
