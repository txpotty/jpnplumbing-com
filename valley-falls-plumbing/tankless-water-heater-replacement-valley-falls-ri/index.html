    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Valley Falls, RI

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. We replace failed units without shutting down the rest. Health code sets the hot water temperature at the hand sinks and dish areas.

    Electric Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Valley Falls, RI

    Electric tankless water heaters skip the gas line entirely, but a whole-house model can need 120 to 160 amps on two to four double-pole breakers. Plenty of panels do not have room. Panel size and wire gauge get checked first.

    Commercial Tankless Racks in Valley Falls, RI

    Restaurants and salons run tankless racks hard. Four units together can pull over 600,000 BTU, which means checking gas pressure with all of them running. We work after hours when needed.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Valley Falls, RI

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. The catch is gas, since most tankless units need a 3/4-inch line or bigger plus new venting. We check the meter size and pipe run before quoting, and we cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer needed. Expect a full day.

    Leaking Tankless Replacement in Valley Falls

    A leaking tankless can drip inside the cabinet for weeks before anyone notices the puddle. Close the cold isolation valve and the gas cock first. We then find out whether it is a fitting or the heat exchanger, since one is cheap and the other is replacement.

    Recirculation Tankless Upgrades in Valley Falls, RI

    Waiting a minute for hot water at the far bathroom wastes thousands of gallons a year in a long single-story house. Navien A models and Rinnai units with internal pumps can recirculate through a return line or a crossover valve under the farthest sink. We set a schedule. Usually just the morning and evening peaks.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Valley Falls, RI

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Tankless vs. Tank Replacement Cost in Valley Falls, RI

    Tankless costs more up front than a tank, often twice as much installed. Over 20 years it can come out close, since a tankless can outlast two tanks and skips standby loss, but the payback depends on how much hot water your house uses. We price both.
