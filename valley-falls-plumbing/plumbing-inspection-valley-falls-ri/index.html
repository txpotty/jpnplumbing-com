    Commercial Property Plumbing Checks in Valley Falls

    Businesses in Valley Falls use our inspections to plan maintenance instead of reacting to breakdowns.     Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Valley Falls, RI

    Older homes in Valley Falls often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. Our camera shows each defect, and the onboard counter tells us exactly how far from the cleanout it sits. We mark it on the ground.     Preventive Maintenance Checks in Valley Falls, RI

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems. The visit covers flushing the heater, working the shutoffs, reading pressure and checking for slow drains and small leaks. Plan on an hour or so. Yearly or every-other-year checks stretch heater life and keep valves from seizing.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Valley Falls

    Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a common sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. Then we explain your options. A softener, a filter or a scale-reducing system can extend heater and fixture life, and we tell you which one fits the hardness we measured.
