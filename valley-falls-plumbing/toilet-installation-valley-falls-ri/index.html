    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Valley Falls, RI

    New Construction Toilet Installation in Valley Falls

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. A flange belongs flush with or just above finished flooring. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Valley Falls

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. We shut off the stop, drain the tank and pull the old bowl. After scraping the old wax off, we check the flange for cracks, set new stainless bolts, and lower the new toilet straight down onto a fresh seal so it compresses evenly. The old one leaves with us.

    Comfort-Height and ADA Toilets in Valley Falls

    Aging in place usually starts in the bathroom. A grab bar and a taller bowl help a lot.     Smart Toilet and Bidet Seat Installation in Valley Falls

    Bidet seats like the TOTO Washlet and Bio Bidet bolt onto most existing elongated bowls. A T-valve splits water off the fill line.     Comfort Height Toilet Installation in Valley Falls, RI

    Comfort height bowls stand about two inches taller than standard ones. Consider who uses the bathroom first. Kids and shorter adults may not like their feet off the floor, but people with sore knees or a new hip usually love the extra height. Installation is the same as a standard bowl.

    Toilet Flange and Wax Ring Replacement in Valley Falls, RI

    The closet flange is the ring that ties the toilet to the drain, and it is the weak point on most old installs. On cast iron, the bolt slots rust out first. Plastic flanges crack from overtightened bolts, and a stainless repair ring screwed over the old one usually gives a solid anchor again. Seal goes on last.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Valley Falls

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. The tank goes on before the bowl is set. Even tightening, alternating sides, matters more than force, since uneven pressure either cracks the china or leaves one bolt weeping. It should not wobble.
