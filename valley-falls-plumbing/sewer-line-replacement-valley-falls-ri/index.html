    What Goes Into a Sewer Lateral Replacement in Valley Falls, RI

    How Pipe Bursting Works in Valley Falls

    A winch pulls the bursting head. Because the head is a little wider than the old pipe, it cracks it into the surrounding dirt as the new HDPE follows. You get a pit at each end. With no joints in the new pipe, roots have no way back in.

    Fixing a Bellied Sewer by Trench in Valley Falls, RI

    A belly is a low spot that holds water. Trenchless methods ride the old pipe, sag and all, which means a real belly has to be dug out and relaid on grade. Rock adds time. We price that in after the locate shows depth. Joints are inspected by us before the city does.

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Valley Falls

    No replacement quote without video. The camera goes in at the cleanout and travels out to the main, recording every crack, offset and root ball as it goes. A sonde in the camera head lets us mark the depth on the lawn. That one step decides between a spot repair and a full run.

    Commercial Sewer Line Replacement in Valley Falls

    Restaurants and strip centers cannot close for a week. We plan commercial sewer replacements around business hours, stage the equipment the night before and often work after close so the line is back in service by opening. Expect six inch pipe, sometimes larger. Heavy grease loads and high flow affect the pipe choice and the cleanout spacing.

    How We Price Sewer Replacement in Valley Falls

    Length and depth drive the price. A short, shallow run through grass is a very different job from a long, deep one under concrete. Method matters too. We break out labor, material, equipment, permits and restoration on the estimate. The total is what you pay.

    Sewer Permits and Code in Valley Falls, RI

    Sewer replacement nearly always needs a permit. We file it, schedule the inspection and meet the inspector on site, so you never have to take a morning off to stand in the yard. The inspector looks at pipe size, slope, bedding and cleanouts. Skipping the permit can come back to bite you at resale.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Valley Falls

    A collapsed run or a long belly usually means we dig. We open a trench along the old line, pull the broken pipe and lay new PVC on compacted bedding at a steady fall of an eighth to a quarter inch per foot. Sod gets cut and rolled aside first when the lawn allows it. The inspector sees open pipe. We compact the backfill in layers, which keeps a dip from forming along the trench.

    Replacing a Collapsed Sewer by Trench in Valley Falls

    Collapsed pipe cannot be burst or lined. When the camera stops at a pile of broken clay or a flattened section of Orangeburg, the only fix is to dig down, clear the debris and lay new pipe through that stretch. Digging also lets us see the soil around the break. Often there is a void or a root mat right at the failure point.
