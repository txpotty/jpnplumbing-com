    What We Fix on Faucets in Valley Falls, RI

    Pull-Down Kitchen Faucet Repair in Valley Falls

    Pull-down kitchen faucets wear at the hose, the spray head and the docking magnet or counterweight that pulls the hose back into the spout. The hose usually splits near the quick-connect fitting. We replace hoses, spray heads and weights with brand parts, then check for drips under the sink where the hose connects.

    Leaking Faucet Handle and Base Repair in Valley Falls, RI

    Water seeping around the handle or pooling at the base usually means failed O-rings on the spout or cartridge, or a mounting nut that has worked loose under the sink. Check the cabinet floor. We replace the O-rings and tighten the mounting hardware, then reseal the base so water on the counter cannot get under it.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Valley Falls

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Bathroom Faucet Repair in Valley Falls, RI

    Bathroom sinks see less wear than kitchen sinks, but scale still builds up in cartridges, and pop-up drains break at the pivot rod under the sink. Neither one takes long. We match cartridges for widespread, centerset and single-hole faucets, and pop-up assemblies are replaced to fit the finish.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Valley Falls, RI

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. We carry commercial cartridges. We set metering faucets to run long enough for a full rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Valley Falls, RI

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. Ask us to check. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard each use proprietary cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Valley Falls, RI

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. The replacement handle gets matched to both the stem and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Valley Falls

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. It shows up on the bill. The worn washer or cartridge gets tracked down and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.
