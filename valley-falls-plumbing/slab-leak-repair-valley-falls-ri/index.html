    Cold Water Slab Leak Repair in Valley Falls

    Cold leaks don't make warm spots. Watch the bill instead. They show up as a higher bill, a meter that turns with every faucet off, or a patch of carpet that stays damp, and they soak the soil just as much as hot leaks do. Your Valley Falls home is no exception. We isolate the cold line and repair or reroute it.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Valley Falls, RI

    Direct repair is often the most efficient fix for a single leak. A small section of slab gets cut. Once the pipe is exposed, we replace the bad section, test the line and backfill in compacted lifts before patching. The concrete is patched flush with the floor.

    Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Valley Falls

    Old galvanized or thin copper is hard to patch reliably. A reroute bypasses it. We abandon the leaking run in place and install new pipe through walls or the attic, so the problem section is gone from service and the water travels through fresh line that you can reach later. Valley Falls homes with original plumbing see this often.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Repair in Valley Falls, RI

    Hot lines leak under slabs more than cold lines. Heat is the reason. The pipe swells and shrinks against rock every time hot water flows, and a recirculation pump keeps that going constantly, thinning the wall. We repair or reroute the line and can set the recirculation pump on a timer.

    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Valley Falls

    Locating comes before cutting. We use a ground mic and a thermal camera. The mic picks up the hiss of water escaping through concrete, and the camera shows warm water spreading from a hot line, so together they narrow the leak to a spot about a foot across. We test hot lines, cold lines and drains in your Valley Falls home.

    After-Hours Slab Leak Calls in Valley Falls

    A main line splitting under the slab can lift tile and soak drywall fast. Call as soon as you see it. The crew caps the broken line, restores water elsewhere and begins repairs right away, keeping soil and flooring damage down. We cover all of Valley Falls.

    Ground Radar and Acoustic Locating in Valley Falls

    Ground-penetrating radar shows what is inside the slab before we cut. It finds rebar and cables. Paired with acoustic listening gear, it lets us mark a leak in copper, PEX or galvanized pipe even near busy roads, because the radar fixes the pipe route and the mic confirms the leak. We use both in Valley Falls.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Valley Falls, RI

    Two or three slab leaks in a few years point to the pipe, not bad luck. A repipe may be cheaper long term. PEX bends with the structure, won't corrode and runs overhead, so a whole-house repipe leaves nothing under the slab. It suits many Valley Falls homes.
