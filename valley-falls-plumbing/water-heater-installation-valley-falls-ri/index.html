    Electric Tank Water Heater Installation in Valley Falls, RI

    Without gas service, an electric tank is the usual pick. We match the voltage and breaker size to your panel, and because electric tanks need no vent, they can go in a closet, an attic or a laundry room where a gas unit could not. Closets and attics get a pan. Quiet and simple.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Valley Falls

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical. Clearances, maximum vent lengths and relief valve rules differ by brand, and warranty claims get denied when an installer skipped them. Our crews install to spec. Your warranty stays intact.

    Traditional Tank Water Heater Installation in Valley Falls

    Tank heaters still heat most homes' water, holding 40 to 75 gallons at temperature until you need it. We size the tank by first-hour rating, not just gallons, because that number tells you how much hot water it delivers in a busy morning. Either fuel works. We connect the fuel, set the vent and haul the old tank away.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Valley Falls, RI

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. Our crews set the gas line, the drip leg and the vent connector, then do a draft test at the hood to prove the exhaust is leaving the house. Then we check for leaks. We add an expansion tank if code calls for it.

    Major Brand Water Heater Installation in Valley Falls, RI

    Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith and Noritz are the brands we install most. Every manufacturer sets its own clearance, venting and relief valve rules, and following them is what keeps the warranty good. Our crews know each one.     Electric Water Heater Installation in Valley Falls, RI

    Electric tanks need a 240-volt circuit, usually a 30-amp double-pole breaker with 10-gauge wire. We confirm the breaker and wire size match the new unit before connecting it, since many older homes have the wrong gauge. No venting needed. You get more spots to put it.

    Water Heater Expansion Tank Installation in Valley Falls

    Closed plumbing systems trap the extra volume water gains when it heats. Permits are generally required for water heater replacements, new fixtures, sewer work and remodels that move plumbing. An expansion tank gives that water somewhere to go, so the relief valve stops dripping and pipe joints stop taking pressure spikes every time the burner runs. We pre-charge it to your house pressure. It mounts on the cold inlet.

    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Valley Falls

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. Ceilings are at stake. Relocation to a safer spot is an option.
