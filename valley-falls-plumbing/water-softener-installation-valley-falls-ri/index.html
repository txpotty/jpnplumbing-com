    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Valley Falls, RI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. JPN Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. A metered control valve regenerates only when needed, which keeps salt and water use down. Most effective option.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Valley Falls

    Salt-free conditioners do not soften water, and we say so up front. They change the minerals into crystals that do not stick to pipe walls and heating elements, so scale drops off without adding sodium or using a drain line. Maintenance is light. They suit moderate hardness.
