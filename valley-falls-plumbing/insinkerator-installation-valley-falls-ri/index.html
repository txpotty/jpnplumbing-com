    Upgrading Your InSinkErator in Valley Falls, RI

    Moving up from a 1/3 HP builder unit to a 3/4 HP Evolution changes more than the grind. The new unit is taller. Because of that height, the discharge can end up below the trap arm, and we measure first so we can lower the trap if needed. We also confirm the circuit can carry the larger motor.

    Badger Replacement for Valley Falls Homes

    Builder-grade kitchens almost always started with a Badger, and a worn one usually gets replaced with another. We pull the old unit first. A matching quick-lock mount in good shape can stay, which saves time and keeps a seal that already fits the sink. The new Badger goes up and gets tested.

    Evolution Series Installation in Valley Falls

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger.     Batch Feed vs Continuous Feed in Valley Falls

    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Valley Falls, RI

A deeper bowl pushes the disposal lower, and if the trap arm in the wall was set for a shallow sink, the discharge can end up below it, so we sometimes lower the wall stub. Better to sort that out mid-remodel than after the tile is up.
