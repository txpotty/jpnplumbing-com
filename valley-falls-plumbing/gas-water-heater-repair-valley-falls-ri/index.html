    A thermocouple is a small copper-sheathed rod that sits in the pilot flame and makes a tiny electric current. The current keeps a magnet in the gas valve pulled open. No flame, no current. When the pilot goes out, the magnet lets go and the valve shuts off gas to both the pilot and the burner, which is the safety that keeps raw gas from filling a closet. A worn one is the top cause of pilot outages.
