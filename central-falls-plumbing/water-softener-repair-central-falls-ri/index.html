    Resin Bed Cleaning and Replacement in Central Falls

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Central Falls

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not.     Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Central Falls, RI

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.
