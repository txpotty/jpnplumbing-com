    Toilet Leaking Through the Ceiling in Central Falls, RI

    Water coming through the ceiling under an upstairs bathroom usually traces back to a failed wax ring or a cracked supply nut, and the drywall below holds the evidence. Leave that toilet alone until we look. We pull the bowl, check the flange and reset it on a new seal, then we show you how far the water traveled so you can decide about the ceiling.

    Leaking Toilet Base Repair in Central Falls, RI

    A rocking bowl breaks the wax seal a little more every time someone sits down, and eventually the leak shows up as a stain or a soft spot. On slab floors the flange is set in concrete and can crack if the closet bolts are overtightened. We check it. If the flange is cracked, we add a stainless repair ring or replace it, then reset the toilet on a new seal.

    Cracked Tank and Supply Line Repair in Central Falls, RI

    Braided supply lines last years, but the plastic nut on the toilet end can crack and a bulge in the braid means the inner hose is about to burst. Every visit includes a look at the supply line. Bulges get replaced on the spot. Old vinyl or solid plastic lines get swapped for braided stainless with metal nuts.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Central Falls

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Most commercial toilets use flushometer valves instead of tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. If downtime is a problem, we can come in before you open or after you lock up.

    Older Toilet Brands We Service in Central Falls, RI

    Older toilets from Eljer, Briggs, Crane and Universal Rundle still turn up in homes built before the 1990s, and their flush valves often take parts nobody stocks anymore.     Leaky Toilet Repair in Central Falls

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. If color shows up in the bowl within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. Most of the time a new flapper does it, unless the seat under it is pitted or cracked.

    Clogged Toilet Clearing in Central Falls, RI

    Most clogs sit in the trapway, the curved channel cast into the porcelain, and a closet auger with a rubber-sleeved head clears them without scratching the bowl. Plungers miss some. When the auger finds nothing and the bowl still fills, the blockage is further down the drain or in the main line. At that point we run a cable from the nearest cleanout or send a camera down to see it.

    Flush Valve Seat Repair in Central Falls
