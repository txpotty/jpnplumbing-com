    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbing Inspection in Central Falls

    Standard plumbing inspections typically run $150 to $300. With the sewer camera, most totals land between $250 and $500.

    Our plumber explains each issue, how serious it is and what the repair options are. You decide what happens next. We can quote any repairs on the spot or later, and you can have us do the work, use another plumber or hand the report to the seller during negotiations.

    We inspect all the common brands and materials. Heaters from every major maker are covered. We work with PEX, copper, CPVC, PVC, cast iron, galvanized and poly, and we know the usual weak points for each and for common fixture brands.

    Watch for a water bill that jumps for no reason, low pressure, slow drains or brown water. Gurgling drains count too. A damp wall, a musty cabinet or a warm spot on the floor can mean a hidden leak, and an inspection finds it before it spreads.

    Our full inspection covers supply pipe, drains, fixtures, shutoff valves, the water heater, gas connectors and the sewer line. We read static pressure. A meter leak test, a check for slab leak signs and hard water damage and a look at any softener or filter are also included.

    You can do a basic check by looking under sinks and watching your water meter. The tools make the difference. Professionals bring sewer video, pressure readings, leak tracing and code knowledge, which find what a visual look misses.

    A standard home inspection takes about one and a half to three hours. Camera work takes longer. Big houses, long sewers or a leak that needs tracking can stretch it, and we give you a time estimate when you schedule.

    Looking for drips and watching the meter is worth doing on your own. It just goes only so far. A plumber's tools reach the sewer and the pipe inside walls, which is where the costly problems usually are.
