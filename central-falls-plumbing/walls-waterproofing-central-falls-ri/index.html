    Drainage System Integration in Central Falls, RI

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Exterior footing drains, interior drain tile and surface French drains all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a downspout emptying right at the foundation. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Shower Pan Leak Repair and Waterproofing in Central Falls, RI

    Wet baseboards outside the shower or a stain on the ceiling below usually point to the pan. We plug the drain, fill the pan with an inch or two of water, and mark the waterline and check it a day later. A drop means a leak. Rebuilding means a mortar pre-slope of a quarter inch per foot, a new PVC liner or bonded membrane, and a clamping drain tightened onto the liner. The new pan gets its own flood test before tile.

    Laundry Room and Utility Wall Waterproofing in Central Falls, RI

    Rubber washing machine hoses burst. We swap them for braided stainless hoses, put a drain pan under the washer piped to a floor drain where one is available, and install a quarter-turn shutoff or an automatic leak valve on the supply box. Water heaters get a pan too. Behind the heater we can install moisture-resistant board and seal it.

    Foundation Crack Repair in Central Falls

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack. We set injection ports every 8 to 12 inches along the crack, seal the surface with epoxy paste, and pump in resin from the bottom port up until it pushes out the next port. Polyurethane flexes a bit if the wall shifts, and epoxy bonds the two sides back together. The surface paste gets ground off once it cures.

    Exterior Foundation Waterproofing in Central Falls

    Exterior waterproofing means digging down to the footing and coating the outside of the wall. It is the most reliable fix for a leaking basement, because the water never touches the block or concrete. We apply a rubberized asphalt or polymer membrane, add a dimple board to protect it, and set perforated drain pipe in gravel along the footing to carry the water away from the wall. Basements are standard in most homes, and many rely on sump pumps to manage groundwater during wet seasons. We backfill after the drain is tested.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Central Falls

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. A long heating season keeps boilers and water heaters working hard, so yearly maintenance helps prevent winter breakdowns. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Central Falls

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. We inject them with low-pressure polyurethane foam, which expands to fill the crack from the inside face to the soil side, or with epoxy when the crack also needs structural bonding. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.

    Interior Walls Waterproofing in Central Falls, RI

    Interior drain tile handles water that already got through the wall. We cut a channel in the slab along the perimeter, lay perforated pipe in washed stone, and run it to a sump basin so the pump can push the water outside. The wall still gets wet. The difference is that the water no longer ends up on your floor. This route costs less than digging outside and makes sense when a patio or driveway sits against the foundation.

    Look low on the wall first. Damp patches, a musty smell, mold on the bottom of the drywall, white powdery efflorescence on block or brick, and puddles after rain all point to water coming through. Bowing walls and stair-step cracks need a closer look too.

    Anywhere from an afternoon to over a week. Interior crack sealing might take a day or two, while exterior excavation with new footing drains might run several days or longer than a week depending on the size of the house. Rain delays exterior work.

    Done right with good materials, a waterproofing system lasts 20 to 50 years.     Damp proofing is usually a thin spray-on asphalt coat. It slows soil moisture but will not stop water that is pushing against the wall under pressure.
