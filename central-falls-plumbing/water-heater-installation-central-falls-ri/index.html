    Attic and Closet Water Heater Installs in Central Falls

    Attic and closet installs take more planning than a garage swap. An attic heater needs a pan with its own drain line, a properly routed relief line and enough access to get the old tank out and the new one in. A leak lands on drywall. If moving it makes sense, we can relocate the heater.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Central Falls, RI

    Going tankless means the heater fires only when a tap opens, and it never runs out. Most gas tankless units need a 3/4 inch gas line and their own PVC or stainless vent, which is different from what a tank uses. They cost more to install. We check gas capacity before quoting.

    Gas Water Heater Installation in Central Falls, RI

    Gas heaters recover faster than electric, often refilling a 50 gallon tank in under an hour. We install the gas line, the sediment trap and the vent connector, then check draft at the hood with a match or a smoke pencil to confirm exhaust is going up and out. Then we check for leaks. Expansion tanks go in where code requires.

    Hybrid Heat Pump Water Heater Installation in Central Falls, RI

    Heat pump water heaters pull warmth from the surrounding air and can use about a third of the power of a standard electric tank. A hybrid needs open air space, often around 700 cubic feet, plus a condensate line to a drain, which rules out most small closets. Some qualify for tax credits. We will tell you if your space works.

    Brand-Specific Water Heater Installation in Central Falls

    Each manufacturer publishes its own install rules, and they are not identical.     Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Central Falls, RI

    Expansion Tank Installation in Central Falls, RI

    A pressure reducing valve or check valve on the main turns your plumbing into a closed system. Heated water then has no way back to the street, so pressure rises and works on the relief valve, supply lines and toilet fill valves. An expansion tank absorbs it. We pre-charge it to match your water pressure.
