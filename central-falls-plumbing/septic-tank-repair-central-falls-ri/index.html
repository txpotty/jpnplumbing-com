    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Central Falls, RI

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones get eaten by sulfuric acid from sewer gas and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Septic Pump and Alarm Repair in Central Falls

    Pumped systems move effluent uphill or to a far field, and the pump sits in its own chamber with a float tree and an alarm. The alarm sounds because liquid climbed past the high float. We check the breaker and meter the pump draw before replacing anything. Zoeller and Liberty parts ride on the truck.

    Drain Field Repair in Central Falls, RI

    Green stripes over the trenches and a wet lawn in dry weather are the classic signs of a field in trouble. The cause is usually biomat, a black layer of slime that seals the soil. Compaction and hydraulic overload from a running toilet can do it too. We test before recommending anything big. Resting or jetting can bring a field back.

    Emergency Septic Repair in Central Falls

    Septic Tee Repair in Central Falls

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet can crack or get knocked off by the hose on a pump truck. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Central Falls

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

Split tanks get replaced.
