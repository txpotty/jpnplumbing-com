    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Central Falls

    Hurricanes and nor'easters can bring heavy rain, storm surge and coastal flooding to Rhode Island, damaging basement water heaters and boilers. A primary pump on household current does nothing once the breaker panel goes dark. The fix is a second, 12-volt pump on its own float, set a little higher than the main pump and wired to a charger and deep-cycle battery. It also covers a stuck primary switch. The alarm will tell you which one is running.

    Backup Pump Options in Central Falls, RI

    Hurricanes and nor'easters can bring heavy rain, storm surge and coastal flooding to Rhode Island, damaging basement water heaters and boilers. Outages tend to hit right when the rain is heaviest and the pit is filling fastest. A backup takes over when the main pump quits or loses power. Where water pressure is strong, a water-powered backup runs off the supply line with no battery at all, protected by a backflow preventer. They will not work on a well.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Replacement in Central Falls

    Basement bathrooms below the sewer line drain into a sealed, vented basin, and the ejector pump in that basin has to pass 2-inch solids. That is not a sump pump job. The ejector and check valve get replaced, then we reseal the lid and check the vent connection. Leaky lids let sewer gas into the basement.

    Sizing a Submersible Sump Pump in Central Falls

    Horsepower matters less than people think. A third-horse pump moves plenty of water for most homes, as long as the vertical lift to the discharge is under about 15 feet. We check the flow chart against your actual lift before we pick a size. Oversize it and it short cycles.

    Commercial Sump Pump Replacement in Central Falls

    Commercial sumps run harder than residential ones, and a restaurant or office basement often has a duplex system with two pumps that alternate on a controller. We replace the pumps and the alternating relay. We can do the work after hours so your tenants or staff are not walking around an open pit.

    Float Switch and Check Valve Replacement in Central Falls

    Stuck floats and leaking check valves cause more sump failures than dead motors do. A check valve that leaks lets the discharge column fall back into the pit, so the pump runs again for the same water and wears itself out early. New valve every time. In a tight pit we use a vertical switch, and in a wide pit a tethered float.

    Pedestal Pumps for Shallow Pits in Central Falls, RI

    Older basements sometimes have a narrow, shallow pit poured right into the floor, and a pedestal pump is often the only thing that fits without breaking concrete. We swap the pump, replace the float rod and stops, and run new 1.5-inch PVC to the check valve. They usually cost less than a submersible.

    Zoeller Switch and Pump Repair in Central Falls

    Zoeller units with a piggyback float plug the pump cord into the switch cord, and if that piggyback plug fails the pump never gets power. That is the first thing we test. Some calls only need a new switch, while others need the whole pump. You will know which before we quote a pump.
