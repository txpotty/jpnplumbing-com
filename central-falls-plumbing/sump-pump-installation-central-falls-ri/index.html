    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Central Falls

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. Lids close flat over them. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Central Falls

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. A flooded elevator pit shuts down a building. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Central Falls

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. Sizing comes from two measurements we take on site, the pit diameter and the vertical lift to the discharge. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Central Falls, RI

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. The charger keeps the battery topped off. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. They matter most below finished rooms.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Central Falls, RI

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Central Falls

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Landlords like them. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Central Falls, RI

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Central Falls

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. They fit pits where two separate pumps would crowd each other. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.
