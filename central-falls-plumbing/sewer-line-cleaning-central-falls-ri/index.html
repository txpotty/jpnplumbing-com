    Sewer Line Cleaning Cost Compared to Replacement in Central Falls, RI

    Cleaning costs a few hundred dollars. Replacement costs thousands. A sound pipe is worth maintaining, while a collapsed or sagging one may need repair, and the camera tells us which, and you see both prices.
