    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Warwick

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in Rhode Island houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers.     Sewer Line Camera Inspection in Warwick, RI

    Older homes in Warwick often have cast iron or clay sewer pipe that cracks, scales up or lets roots in at the joints. The camera records every defect along with its distance from the cleanout.     Seasonal Plumbing Checks in Warwick, RI

    Cold winters and humid summers give Rhode Island plumbing a full range of seasonal stress over the course of a year. A seasonal check looks at the parts of the system that weather affects most. We look at hose bibbs, exposed pipe in garages and crawlspaces, irrigation backflow devices and the water heater's relief valve and drain. Insulation gets checked too. Swapping a cracked outdoor faucet or insulating a pipe costs little next to a burst line in the wall.

    Rental and Commercial Property Inspections in Warwick

    Landlords and property managers use our inspections between tenants or before buying an investment property. We document each unit.     Plumbing Inspection Costs in Warwick, RI

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Warwick

    Blue-green stains on sinks and tubs are a frequent sign of corrosive water, and treatment can protect the plumbing. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Pipe Material and Water Quality Review in Warwick, RI

    Pipe material says a lot about what to expect from a house. Our plumber identifies the supply pipe, whether copper, CPVC, PEX, galvanized, poly or Kitec, and explains how each usually fails. Certain types need a plan. Polybutylene and Kitec are known for sudden failures, and galvanized slowly closes up with rust, so those get flagged with replacement options.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Warwick, RI

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.
