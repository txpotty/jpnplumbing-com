    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Warwick, RI

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. We stock the common kits. It means most repairs need only one trip.

    Leaking Disposal Fix in Warwick, RI

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out. We drop the unit, pull the flange, clean off the old putty and reset it with fresh putty and new gaskets, then hang the disposal back up. Most top leaks stop there. A leak on the side is usually the discharge gasket or a loose hose clamp.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Warwick, RI

    Keep cold water running before, during and after use, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Fibrous scraps jam it most. Throw out celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.

    Noise and Odor Diagnosis in Warwick

    A rattle that stops after a few seconds is usually a spoon or a pit bouncing in the chamber. A steady whine is different. A steady whine usually points to failing motor bearings, and the noise keeps building until the motor locks up. Odors usually trace to the splash guard. The guard comes out, the chamber gets cleaned and a torn guard gets replaced.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Warwick

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. We check the controls first. A failed solenoid or a tripped overload in the control panel stops the unit just as completely as a jam, and it is a much faster fix.

    Humming Disposal Repair in Warwick

    Humming with no spin usually means something is stuck. Spoons, glass, bones and pits cause most of them. We cut power, work the flywheel free with a hex wrench on the bottom of the unit, remove the object and then test the motor and the reset button with water running.

    Electrical Issues and Motor Repair in Warwick

    A disposal that hums but will not grind has power but cannot turn, while one that is silent has no power at all. The two need different fixes. We check the breaker, the reset button and the switch for silent units, and the flywheel and motor windings for humming ones, then decide if a repair makes sense.

    After-Hours Disposal Service in Warwick

    A disposal that quits while you are cooking stops everything, especially if the sink is full of water. Do not try drain chemicals. Chemical openers sitting in a disposal can damage the seals and splash back when the plumber opens the trap, so it is better to leave the water and call us.
