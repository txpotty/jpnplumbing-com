    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Warwick, RI

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. JPN Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. Most effective option.

    Water Softener Replacement Services in Warwick, RI

    Worn-out softeners usually show it in one of two ways, hard water at the tap or a brine tank that never drops. The work is usually done in a few hours.
