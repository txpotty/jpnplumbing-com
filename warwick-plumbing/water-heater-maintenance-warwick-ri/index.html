    What a Water Heater Tune-Up Covers in Warwick, RI

    Tank Flush and Inspection in Warwick

    Sediment forms from minerals in the water. We flush the tank, test the relief valve, check the anode and look over each fitting. The tank heats better. Recovery speeds up.

    Electric Element Service in Warwick, RI

    Two elements, two thermostats. A burned-out lower element is the usual cause of running short on hot water. We test with power off. We only replace what failed.

    Flushing a Tank Water Heater in Warwick

    Popping and rumbling mean sediment. Scale on the tank bottom traps heat and causes water to pop and rumble as it heats. We flush until it runs clear. Heavy buildup may take several cycles.

    Attic, Closet and Garage Units in Warwick

    Location changes the risks. Attic units need a working drain pan and drain line so a leak does not come through the ceiling, and garage gas units often need to be raised off the floor or protected from vehicles under local code. We check the pan and drain. We check venting and access.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Warwick

    Electric tanks fail at the lower element. Sediment buries the lower element until it overheats and fails, leaving you with a short supply of hot water. Both elements get tested. Thermostats and wiring get checked with the power off.

    Tankless Water Heater Service in Warwick, RI

    Tankless units need descaling. Mineral scale coats the inside of the heat exchanger, restricting flow and making the unit work harder, and eventually it starts throwing error codes or cutting out in the middle of a shower. A pump circulates descaler through it. Igniter, fan and exhaust venting get checked too.

    Water Heater Leak Checks in Warwick, RI

    Small drips become floods. We inspect the supply connections, the relief valve outlet, the drain valve and the tank seams for moisture or rust trails, and we check the drain pan and the floor around the unit for signs of a slow leak. Rust trails mean trouble. Early repairs protect floors and drywall.
