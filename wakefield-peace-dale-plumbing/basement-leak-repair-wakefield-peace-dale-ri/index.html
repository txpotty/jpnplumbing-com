    Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing covers the whole wall. It handles groundwater all around the foundation and makes sense when water comes in at many points.

    Some small leaks are fine to handle yourself, like tightening a washer hose or replacing a leaking supply connector.     Gutters and downspouts do most of the work. Keep them clear and extended well away from the foundation. Test the sump each spring and swap rubber washer hoses for braided ones every five years.

    Only some repairs need excavation. Exterior waterproofing and footing drain replacement mean digging down to the footing along the leaking wall.
