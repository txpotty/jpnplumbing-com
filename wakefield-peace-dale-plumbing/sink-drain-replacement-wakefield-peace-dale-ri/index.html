    Kitchen Sink Drain Replacement in Wakefield-Peace Dale

    Kitchen drains start failing at the basket strainer. The putty under a 3-1/2 inch strainer dries out, the locknut rusts, and water seeps down the outside of the tailpiece where it looks like the trap is leaking even when it is not. Strainer and trap get swapped as a pair. Disposal sinks get a new flange instead.

    Choosing Drain Material in Wakefield-Peace Dale, RI

    PVC is the default choice under most sinks. It does not rust, it stands up to drain cleaners, and it costs less than brass, while polished 17-gauge brass makes sense under a pedestal sink or any trap you can see. ABS works the same way. We match what you have, with a proper transition where they meet.

    Utility Sink Drain Replacement in Wakefield-Peace Dale

    Laundry tubs see lint and paint water. Over time that wears the thin plastic drain and trap on a utility sink until it cracks, and lint from the washer standpipe often builds up in the trap so badly that it never drains at full speed again. We fit a 1-1/2 inch trap with a cleanout. A lint trap on the washer hose helps.

    Leaking P-Trap Replacement in Wakefield-Peace Dale

    Traps leak at the joints first. Slip nut washers flatten and harden with age, and a trap opened often for clogs usually has stripped threads. Trap kits are cheap. Labor is most of the bill.

    Corroded Drain Replacement in Wakefield-Peace Dale, RI

    Drain cleaners speed up corrosion. Caustic products sit in the bottom of a metal trap between uses and eat at the chrome plating and the thin brass underneath, which is why so many old traps we pull have pinholes right at the bottom of the bend. PVC does not have that problem. We recommend it for most cabinets.

    Bathroom Sink Drain Replacement in Wakefield-Peace Dale, RI

    Bathroom drains wear out at the pop-up. The pivot nut packing leaks, the drain body corrodes around the overflow holes, and the stopper stops sealing, so a sink that will not hold water usually needs a whole new pop-up assembly. Half-fixes do not hold here. The new drain matches your faucet finish.

They open and close with a press on the cap, and we check that the sink has an overflow before choosing a slotted body, since a slotted drain in a bowl without an overflow leaks out the slots. They clean easier too. Hair still collects, so pull the cap now and then.
