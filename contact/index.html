    The fastest way to reach a plumber

    Call (401) 364-2507. A real person answers around the clock, confirms your address, and dispatches the nearest licensed plumber. For emergencies such as burst pipes, sewage backups, gas smells, or no water, call rather than email so we can respond immediately.

    Office hours

    Monday - Saturday : 8.00 am - 6.00 pm. Emergency service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays.

    Email

    For non-urgent questions, estimates, or commercial maintenance plans, email info@jpnplumbing.com. We reply within one business day.

    Where we work

    JPN Plumbing serves 16 cities across Rhode Island and the communities within roughly 40 miles of each. Find your city to see local services and information, or call and we will confirm coverage for your address.

    What to have ready when you call

    • Your address and the best callback number
    • A short description of the problem and when it started
    • Whether water or gas is currently shut off
    • Photos, if you have them, which you can text after we answer

    Licensing and insurance

    Every JPN Plumbing plumber is licensed in Rhode Island, insured, and background-checked. We pull permits where required and provide written, flat-rate pricing before any work begins.
