    PEX and Copper Water Line Repair in Westerly

    Copper and PEX fail in different places. Copper tends to pinhole where a rock rubs it or where the water chemistry eats the wall from inside. PEX usually fails at a fitting. We cut out the bad section of copper and sweat or press in a new length, while PEX gets a fresh crimp ring or expansion fitting with the right tool for the brand.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Westerly

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Newer homes and remodels around Westerly typically use copper or PEX supply lines, which are easier to maintain. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. Acoustic gear and a thermal camera tell us where it is. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. We price each one for you.
