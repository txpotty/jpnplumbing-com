    Damaged Baffle Repair in Westerly

    Baffles are the walls inside the trap that slow the water down long enough for grease to float and solids to sink.     Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Westerly

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean.
