    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Westerly

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. We usually pair that with a 1-1/2 inch discharge line.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Westerly

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. Because the motor is cooled by the surrounding water, they run quieter and can push more gallons per minute up a tall lift than most pedestal pumps. Lids close flat over them. We seal the cord grommet and the lid on every install.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Westerly

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Rentals need these. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Westerly, RI

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Crawl Space Sump Pump Installation in Westerly, RI

    Crawl spaces with standing water breed mold and rot sill plates within a few seasons. We set a basin at the lowest point, run perforated drain pipe to it where needed and seal the vapor barrier around the lid so the space stays dry. Clearance is usually under two feet. We route the discharge out through the rim joist and away from the foundation.
