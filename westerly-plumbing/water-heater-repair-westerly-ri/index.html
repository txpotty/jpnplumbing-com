    Rusty hot water usually means corrosion inside the tank. The anode rod may be used up, or the tank lining may be failing. Replacing the anode helps if the tank is sound, but a rusting lining means the end is close.

    A leak from a valve or fitting is urgent but usually fixable. A leak from the tank itself can worsen quickly, so close the cold supply, kill the fuel and call.
