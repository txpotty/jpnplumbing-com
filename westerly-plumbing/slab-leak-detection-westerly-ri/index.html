    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Westerly

    Water escaping a pressurized pipe makes a hiss that carries up through concrete. Our ground microphones hear it. We move the pickup across the floor in a grid over the traced pipe route, finding where the sound is loudest and confirming it from another position, which works best on copper and less well on PEX.

    Repiping After Repeat Slab Leaks in Westerly, RI

    Two slab leaks usually means more are coming. The rest of the pipe has likely aged the same way, so repiping replaces every supply line with new PEX or copper through the walls and attic, ending the cycle of floor openings and surprise water bills. Most repipes wrap up in two to four days.

    Listening Through Concrete in Westerly

    Ground microphones are sensitive. That means appliances, HVAC fans and traffic all show up in the headphones, so our techs shut off what they can and learn to tune out the rest, which is a skill that takes years to get good at. Pressurized lines are the easiest to hear in your Westerly home.

    Supply and Drain Leaks Under the Slab in Westerly, RI

    Supply leaks and drain leaks act differently. Supply leaks are pressurized, so they move the meter, warm the floor on the hot side and run whether or not anyone uses water. Drain leaks only leak when water goes down, showing up as smells, sluggish drains or wet spots near bathrooms. Each needs its own test in your Westerly, RI home.

    Thermal Scans on Large Floors in Westerly, RI

    Large open floors can hide a leak. A thermal camera scans an entire living area in a few minutes, showing temperature differences of a degree or less across tile or concrete, which lets us rule out large areas before acoustic work starts. It saves an hour on large homes across Westerly, RI.

    Hot Water Slab Leak Detection in Westerly

    The hot side fails more than the cold. Heat speeds up corrosion, and the pipe expands and contracts every time someone showers, rubbing against concrete and gravel until it wears through. Warm floors and a water heater that runs nonstop are the signs. Energy bills often rise.

    Cold Water Slab Leak Detection in Westerly, RI

    Cold-side leaks leave no warm spot. That makes them harder to spot. They usually show up as a running meter, damp carpet, a musty smell or water seeping at baseboards, which is why we isolate the cold line, trace its route and listen along it with acoustic gear.
