    Pressure-Assisted Toilet Installation in Westerly

    Pressure-assisted toilets have a sealed vessel inside the tank that uses line pressure to push the flush. They need at least 20 psi to work right. Before suggesting one, we put a gauge on a hose bib, and we mention the noise, because the flush carries through the whole house. They rarely clog.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Westerly, RI

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.
